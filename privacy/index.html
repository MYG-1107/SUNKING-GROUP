Privacy notice

How this website is designed to handle information and what to check before enabling online enquiries.

Last updated: October 2026

About this notice

This notice describes the intended operation of the SUNKING GROUP website. The site is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. This notice should be reviewed and approved by the organisation's responsible person before production use.

Information you enter

The contact form asks for your name, email address, an optional phone number, the subject of your enquiry and the message. The form opens a draft email in the visitor’s configured email application. The visitor must review and send that draft. The static website itself does not send or store the message. If the visitor sends it, their email provider and the organisation’s mailbox may process the submitted information.

Form service and retention

If a third-party form service is connected, the organisation should update this notice to identify the service provider, purposes of processing, data location where relevant, retention period, security controls and contact route for privacy requests. Do not collect sensitive personal information through a general enquiry form.

Hosting and technical logs

The hosting provider may process technical request information as part of operating and securing the website. Refer to the hosting provider's current privacy terms for details of its handling of server logs and security information.

Cookies and analytics

This website loads the Google Tag Manager container GTM-PV8P6DSV. Tag Manager is used to manage scripts and measurement tags configured by authorised site administrators. Depending on which tags are enabled and published in that container, technologies such as Google Analytics may process information such as pages visited, interactions, referring pages, browser and device information, and cookie or similar identifiers. The container snippet alone does not establish which analytics tags are active; actual collection depends on the configuration published in Tag Manager. Google's privacy information is available at Google Privacy Policy. Before enabling analytics or advertising tags that use cookies or similar identifiers, SUNKING GROUP should review applicable privacy and consent requirements and configure an appropriate consent mechanism where required.

External links

This website links to external organisations and professional profiles. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Your choices and requests

For privacy questions or requests relating to this website, email contact@sunkinggroup.org. The organisation should confirm and publish any jurisdiction-specific rights and procedures that apply.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated when site functionality, contact arrangements or legal requirements change.

The organisation should review this notice whenever the Tag Manager container changes, and keep any analytics, advertising or consent settings aligned with the privacy choices and legal requirements that apply to site visitors.
