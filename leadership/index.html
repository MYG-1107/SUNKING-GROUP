Leadership philosophy

Learning as the foundation of leadership

Prof. Dr. Chaitanya Niphadkar is an academic leader, author and edupreneur whose stated work brings together experiential learning, people development and organisational change. His areas of focus include leadership, human resources, organisational development and change management, with an emphasis on helping institutions and teams build capability.

His leadership approach emphasises continuous development, reflection and real-world application. The “Champ Performer” concept is presented as a way of positioning individuals as change agents who contribute to organisational performance, innovation and team effectiveness. His approach draws on transformational and servant leadership, with emphasis on emotional intelligence, strategic thinking, collaboration and a human-centred approach to change.

Academic and professional profile

Published biographical materials describe Prof. Niphadkar as a Lancaster University alumnus, author of eight books, a research contributor and a supervisor of doctoral (PhD and DBA) candidates. They list a post-doctoral research fellowship, three doctoral degrees, two master's degrees and certifications in education and technology, along with professional memberships associated with the American Management Association, SHRM and CIPD UK. The profile also describes international experience across the United Kingdom, United States, Switzerland, India, East Africa and the United Arab Emirates.

Those materials describe him as a “Verified Educator” with Harvard Business Impact and a member of the Harvard Business School Online academic community. They also refer to a UK patent for a research design and the “Champ Performer” concept. Current qualifications, roles, awards and memberships should be confirmed from the relevant issuing organisations or official profiles before these details are relied upon.

Teaching and research interests

Leadership, human resources, organisational behaviour, learning and development, change management and sustainability / sustainable development.
