Experiential learning approach

The Institute’s published pedagogy draws on David Kolb’s experiential learning model. Rather than relying only on passive listening, experiential learning connects experience, reflection, the development of concepts and practical experimentation.

The four stages in the model are commonly described as concrete experience, reflective observation, abstract conceptualisation and active experimentation. The approach encourages learners to participate, reflect on what happened, build understanding and apply it to new situations.

Frequently asked questions

Planning an application

When should I apply?

The existing website describes rolling admissions for many programmes and recommends applying around 6–8 weeks before the intended start date. Intake schedules vary, so request the current deadline and start date for the specific programme.

Can previous study or credits be considered?

The source website states that transfer-credit requests may be assessed based on previous transcripts and programme relevance. This is not an automatic entitlement; confirm whether credit transfer is available for the chosen programme and obtain the decision in writing.

How are online classes delivered?

The existing site describes a learning-management-system model that may include asynchronous lectures, readings and assignments, discussion boards, virtual group projects and optional live sessions. Ask for the current delivery details, assessment methods and attendance requirements for the specific course.

What technology might be needed for online study?

The source site lists a reliable desktop or laptop, high-speed internet, webcam and microphone as general requirements. Specific programmes may need additional software; confirm the current list before enrolling.

Are scholarships, grants or payment plans available?

The source site mentions scholarships, grants and payment plans, but eligibility and availability are not confirmed by this page. Do not assume support will be available: request the applicable written terms, eligibility criteria and any conditions before making a financial decision.

Important programme verification: Published programme descriptions and durations are indicative, not an admission offer or guarantee. Before applying or paying fees, obtain written confirmation of the exact programme, awarding institution, delivery mode, entry requirements, total fees, duration, accreditation or recognition status, refund terms and any partner relationship. Do not assume all programmes are currently available.
