## A careful approach to public stories

Testimonials and case studies can help prospective learners and organisations understand what an experience involved. They should also be fair to the people and organisations whose names, words and results are published.

SUNKING GROUP will publish a learner or client story only when the relevant person or organisation has approved the material and the key details can be checked. We will not create placeholder testimonials, invent client names, imply endorsements or publish unverified performance figures.

## What a useful case study should explain

A case study may include:

1. **Context:** the learner’s goal or the organisation’s challenge.
2. **Starting point:** relevant background and the issues identified.
3. **Approach:** the programme, service or activity that was actually delivered.
4. **Evidence:** a documented outcome, with the measurement period and source where a metric is used.
5. **Limitations:** other factors that may have influenced the result.
6. **Permission:** confirmation that the named person or organisation approved the wording, image, logo and publication channel.

A testimonial should use the participant’s own approved words and be attributed only to the level of detail they have consented to share.

## What can be submitted?

A learner may share a course reflection, completed project or professional milestone. A client may propose a case study about a defined engagement. Submissions should not contain sensitive personal information, confidential company details or third-party material that the sender is not authorised to share.

## How verification works

The editorial review should confirm the participant or client relationship, the accuracy of the service description, the basis for any outcome claim and the scope of consent. Metrics should include a clear definition and source. A statement of satisfaction should not be presented as proof of measurable business impact.

**Publication status:** No individual testimonial, learner achievement or client result has been added to this page without supporting material. This page sets out the evidence and consent standard for future stories; it is not itself a claim of a particular result.
