## Rules to review before enrolling

Academic requirements differ between institutions and qualifications. Before accepting an offer, request the current academic regulations and programme handbook. Check that the documents identify the awarding institution and apply to the intake you are joining.

## Key areas

- **Attendance and participation:** minimum attendance, live-session expectations and rules for online participation.
- **Assessment:** assignment formats, examinations, projects, dissertations, submission deadlines and resubmission limits.
- **Grading:** marking scales, pass thresholds, classification rules and moderation.
- **Progression:** prerequisites, module sequencing, repeat requirements and maximum completion periods.
- **Academic integrity:** citation, plagiarism, collusion, use of generative AI and research ethics.
- **Extensions and mitigating circumstances:** how to request an extension and what evidence is required.
- **Appeals and complaints:** the difference between an academic appeal, a service complaint and a request for review.
- **Award and completion:** credit requirements, outstanding fees, graduation and certificate or transcript issue.

## Keep a copy

Save the handbook, offer letter, fee schedule and any written clarification. Where a pathway involves more than one organisation, ask which organisation is responsible for each decision and which complaints or appeal procedure applies.

SUNKING GROUP can help direct an enquiry. The named awarding institution's official regulations take precedence for the qualification.
