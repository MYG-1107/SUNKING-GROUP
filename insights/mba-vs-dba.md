## Start with the question you want to answer

An MBA and a DBA are not interchangeable qualifications. An MBA is generally a postgraduate management degree designed to develop broad business knowledge and managerial capability. A DBA is a professional doctorate that usually places greater emphasis on applied research into a complex business or organisational issue.

The exact curriculum, academic level, award title and admission rules depend on the university and country. Always compare the specific programme regulations rather than relying on the qualification name alone.

## Typical focus

| Area | MBA | DBA |
|---|---|---|
| Main purpose | Develop broad management and business capability | Develop advanced applied research and professional practice |
| Typical audience | Graduates and professionals developing management capability | Experienced professionals seeking doctoral-level inquiry |
| Learning focus | Finance, marketing, operations, people, strategy and leadership | Research design, critical inquiry, applied theory and a substantial research project |
| Evidence of learning | Coursework, cases, projects, exams or a dissertation, depending on the programme | Doctoral-level research and a thesis or equivalent assessment, according to regulations |

This comparison is general. Some MBA programmes include substantial research, and DBA structures vary between institutions.

## Who might consider an MBA?

An MBA may be relevant to a person seeking a structured view of business functions, moving into management, developing strategic understanding or exploring a new business direction. Applicants should check whether their previous degree, professional experience and learning goals match the programme.

## Who might consider a DBA?

A DBA may suit an experienced manager, consultant or professional who wants to investigate a significant practice-based question using rigorous research. It requires sustained independent work, critical engagement with literature, suitable methods and compliance with research ethics.

A DBA is not simply a shorter route to a PhD, and it should not be selected only because it includes the word "doctor". Applicants should understand the institution's research expectations and the recognition of the specific award.

## Questions to ask before enrolling

- Which institution awards the qualification, and what is the exact award title?
- What are the current entry criteria and required professional experience?
- How long does the programme normally take in the chosen study mode?
- What are the total fees, assessment requirements and refund conditions?
- What independent evidence supports any recognition or accreditation claim?
- What academic, research and student-support arrangements are included?

## A sensible next step

Define your objective first: broader management learning, a career transition, advanced professional practice or a research contribution. Then request the official prospectus and compare the exact programmes.
