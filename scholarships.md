## Check current availability

Scholarships, bursaries, grants, employer sponsorship and instalment arrangements are different types of financial support. Their availability, value and eligibility depend on the institution and programme. A general reference to financial assistance should not be treated as a promise that funding is currently open.

## Questions to ask

Request written answers to the following:

- Which programme and intake are eligible?
- Who funds and awards the assistance?
- What academic, financial, geographic or experience criteria apply?
- Is a separate application required, and what is the deadline?
- What documents are needed?
- Does the award cover tuition only, or other charges as well?
- Can the award be combined with another discount or payment plan?
- What happens if a learner defers, withdraws or does not meet progression requirements?

## Plan your budget carefully

Do not commit to a programme based on an expected scholarship until you have received written confirmation from the responsible provider. Ask for the standard total fee and the amount payable if the funding application is unsuccessful.

The [fees and payment page](/fees-and-payment/) explains the costs to confirm before enrolment. Contact the team for programme-specific information.
