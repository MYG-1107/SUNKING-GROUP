## Education and programmes

### Is SUNKING GROUP itself the awarding university?

Do not assume that it is. The responsible awarding institution must be identified in the official programme documents. The role of SUNKING GROUP and the institution should be explained separately.

### How do I know whether a programme is recognised?

Request the exact qualification title, awarding institution and current recognition evidence. Verify the claim with the relevant regulator, awarding institution or professional body. See [accreditations and recognition](/about/recognition/).

### Where can I find admission requirements?

Start with the [admissions page](/admissions/), then request the current entry criteria and document checklist for the specific course and intake.

### Are programme fees published?

A verified current fee table is not published here. Request a dated fee schedule and confirm all additional charges and cancellation terms before payment. See [fees and payment](/fees-and-payment/).

### Are scholarships guaranteed?

No scholarship or funded place is guaranteed by this general information page. Ask the programme provider whether current funding is available and request its eligibility and award terms in writing.

## Training and consulting

### Can corporate training be customised?

The training enquiry process can begin with the organisation's goals, audience, delivery preference and expected outcomes. The scope, trainer allocation, dates and costs must be agreed in a written proposal.

### Does SUNKING GROUP publish a schedule of upcoming events?

Upcoming dates are added to the [training calendar](/training/calendar/) and [events page](/events/) when details have been confirmed. Contact the team about a proposed or private session.

### How does a consulting engagement begin?

A consulting enquiry is reviewed to understand the context and scope. See the [consultancy process](/consulting/process/) for the stages to discuss before agreeing deliverables and fees.

## Contact and policies

### How does the website enquiry form work?

The current static-site form prepares an email draft in the visitor's default email application. The visitor must review and send it. The site does not directly store or submit form entries.

### What should I do if I have a complaint?

Use the official contact channel at [contact@sunkinggroup.org](mailto:contact@sunkinggroup.org) and include the relevant programme or service, dates and supporting details. Where an external provider is responsible, use its formal complaints process as well.

### Where can I find accessibility and cookie information?

See the [accessibility statement](/accessibility/) and [cookie information](/cookies/).
