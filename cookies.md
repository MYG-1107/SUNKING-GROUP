## What is loaded on the website?

The website includes a Google Tag Manager container identified as **GTM-PV8P6DSV**. Tag Manager can load analytics, measurement or other scripts depending on the tags, triggers and consent settings published in the container.

The presence of the container alone does not identify every cookie, data recipient, retention period or purpose. Those details must be checked against the live Tag Manager configuration and a current cookie scan.

## Cookies and similar technologies

Depending on the active configuration, a website may use:

- Essential technologies needed for basic site operation or security.
- Analytics or measurement technologies used to understand site visits and interactions.
- Embedded or third-party technologies that may set their own identifiers.

This page does not claim that each category is currently active. The actual inventory should be published after the production configuration has been reviewed.

## Choices and consent

The current repository does not establish that a cookie-consent banner or consent-management platform is installed. Any consent requirements depend on the technologies used, the user's location and applicable law. The site owner should review the tags and implement an appropriate consent mechanism where required before deploying non-essential tracking.

For privacy questions, email [contact@sunkinggroup.org](mailto:contact@sunkinggroup.org). See the [privacy notice](/privacy/) for the broader information-handling statement.
