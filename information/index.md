## Important before you apply or engage

Programme availability, entry requirements, delivery, assessment, fees, refund conditions and qualification awards can vary by provider and intake. SUNKING GROUP may describe, support or facilitate a pathway, while a separate institution may be responsible for admission, teaching, assessment or awarding a qualification. Always ask for current official documents that name the responsible organisation and clarify its role.

The information published here is general guidance. Where an approved policy, verified staff profile, fee schedule, accreditation record, partner agreement, dated event or completed project has not been supplied for publication, the relevant page says so rather than presenting an unverified claim as fact.
