Visionary leadership

Learning as the foundation of leadership

Prof. Dr. Chaitanya Niphadkar is described on the SUNKING website as an international academic leader, author and edupreneur whose work brings together experiential learning, people development and organisational change. His areas of focus include leadership, human resources, organisational development and change management.

His leadership philosophy emphasises continuous development, reflection and application in real-world settings. The “Champ Performer” concept is presented as a way of positioning people as change agents who contribute to organisational performance, innovation and team effectiveness. His approach draws on transformational and servant leadership, with emphasis on emotional intelligence, strategic thinking, collaboration and a human-centred approach to change.

Academic and professional profile

The existing institutional profile describes Prof. Niphadkar as a Lancaster University alumnus, author of eight books, research contributor and supervisor of doctoral (PhD and DBA) candidates. The profile lists one post-doctoral research fellowship, three doctoral degrees, two master’s degrees and certifications in education and technology, alongside memberships of the American Management Association (AMA), SHRM and CIPD UK. The profile describes international experience across the UK, USA, Switzerland, India, East Africa and the UAE. It lists teaching and research interests in leadership, human resources, organisational behaviour, learning and development, change management and sustainability / sustainable development.
