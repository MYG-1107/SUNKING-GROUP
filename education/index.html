Experiential learning

The group's published pedagogy draws on David Kolb's experiential learning model. Learners develop understanding through direct experience, active participation and reflection rather than passive listening alone. Knowledge is strengthened by transforming experience into insight and applying it to a new situation.

The cycle commonly includes four connected stages:

  1. Concrete experience: engage with a task, challenge or real-life situation.
  2. Reflective observation: review what happened, what worked and what could be improved.
  3. Abstract conceptualisation: connect observations with ideas, concepts and frameworks.
  4. Active experimentation: apply the learning to a new task, decision or professional setting.

This approach encourages learners to engage, reflect, conceptualise and apply knowledge in real-world settings.

Before you apply

Questions to confirm in writing

Are programmes accredited or internationally recognised?

Recognition and accreditation are programme- and awarding-body-specific. Ask for the exact awarding institution, qualification title and current independent evidence of recognition before enrolling. Do not infer recognition of one programme from a provider's general marketing statement.

What are the entry requirements and application deadlines?

Some published programme information described rolling admissions and recommended applying 6–8 weeks before an intended start date. Treat this as indicative only: request the current official deadlines, document checklist, entry criteria and decision timeline.

Can I study online or on campus?

Programme listings include online and campus-based categories. Published online-learning descriptions refer to a learning platform, recorded or asynchronous materials, readings, assignments, discussion boards, virtual group work and optional live sessions. Confirm the actual current format, attendance expectations and assessment rules for the chosen course.

Can previous credits be transferred, and is financial assistance available?

Published information has described credit-transfer assessment and possible scholarships, grants or payment plans for some learners. Availability and eligibility are not guaranteed. Ask the provider for its current written policies, assessment method and financial terms.

Important: Do not rely on a summary page alone to confirm fees, awards, professional recognition, transfer credit or admission eligibility. Request current official documents for the specific programme.
