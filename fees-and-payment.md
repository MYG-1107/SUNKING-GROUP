## Request an official fee schedule

Fees may differ by programme, provider, study mode, country, intake and payment arrangement. A general website price should not be treated as a final offer. Ask for a dated fee schedule or written quotation issued by the organisation responsible for the programme.

## Costs to confirm

Before making a payment, ask whether the total includes:

- Application, registration or enrolment charges.
- Tuition and academic resource fees.
- Examination, assessment, dissertation or project fees.
- Graduation, certificate or transcript charges.
- Technology, platform or library access fees.
- Taxes, currency-conversion charges or bank transfer costs.
- Any travel, accommodation or in-person attendance costs.

Also ask whether fees are payable in instalments, whether a deposit is refundable, the due dates and what happens if a learner defers or withdraws.

## Payment safety

Verify the legal name of the payment beneficiary against the programme agreement or official invoice. Do not transfer money to a personal account or use payment instructions received only through an unverified social-media message. Keep the invoice and payment receipt.

## No general fee promise

A fee, scholarship, discount or payment plan should only be relied upon when its amount, eligibility, expiry date and conditions are confirmed in writing by the responsible provider. Contact the team to request the current documentation for your chosen course.
