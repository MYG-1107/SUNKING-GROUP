## SSBM Geneva tuition fees

SUNKING GROUP is a Business Partner of SSBM Geneva. SSBM Geneva publishes its tuition fees directly and may update them as programmes or payment arrangements change.

For SSBM programmes, use the current [official tuition-fee page](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/) and confirm the amount and currency for the exact programme and delivery format. Do not rely on an old brochure, screenshot or third-party summary as a final quotation.

The institution's published fee information describes payment options and possible instalment or discount arrangements. Eligibility and conditions can differ. Ask SSBM Geneva to confirm any offer in writing before making a payment.

## Other programmes

For programmes provided by another institution, request a dated fee schedule or written quotation from that provider. A general website price should not be treated as a final offer.

## Costs to confirm

Before making a payment, ask whether the total includes:

- Application, registration or enrolment charges.
- Tuition and academic resource fees.
- Examination, assessment, dissertation or project fees.
- Graduation, certificate or transcript charges.
- Technology, platform or library access fees.
- Taxes, currency-conversion charges or bank transfer costs.
- Travel, accommodation or in-person attendance costs.

Also ask whether fees are payable in instalments, whether a deposit is refundable, the due dates and what happens if a learner defers or withdraws.

## Payment safety

Verify the legal name of the payment beneficiary against the programme agreement or official invoice. Pay only through the payment route confirmed by the institution or contracting organisation. SUNKING GROUP does not set SSBM tuition fees and should not be treated as the fee-receiving institution unless an official written agreement specifically says so.

Keep the invoice and payment receipt. Read the relevant [refund and cancellation information](/governance/refunds/) before paying.
