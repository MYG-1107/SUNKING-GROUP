Privacy notice

How this static website handles information shared through the site.

Home / Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This notice is a starter template for the SUNKING GROUP website and should be reviewed by the organisation’s responsible legal or privacy contact before launch.

Information you provide

The contact form on this static website opens the visitor’s default email application with a draft message addressed to SUNKING GROUP. The website itself does not submit the form to a database or store the message. The visitor must send the email to complete the enquiry.

Email correspondence

If you email a SUNKING GROUP address, the information you include will be handled through the organisation’s email systems and retained in accordance with its operational and legal requirements. Do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary and you have been given an appropriate channel to share it.

Cookies and analytics

This package does not include advertising pixels, analytics scripts or non-essential cookies by default. If analytics, embedded media, marketing tools or third-party form services are added later, update this notice and configure any required consent before launch.

Third-party services

Links to external websites are provided for reference. Those websites have their own privacy practices. Review their policies before submitting information to them.

Contact

For questions about this notice, email sunking.group@sunking-institute.com.
