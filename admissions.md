## Before you apply

Admission requirements, application deadlines and selection decisions are set by the institution responsible for the specific programme. Requirements can vary by qualification, subject, delivery mode, country and intake. An enquiry to SUNKING GROUP is not an admission offer or confirmation of eligibility.

## For SSBM Geneva programmes

SUNKING GROUP is a Business Partner of the Swiss School of Business and Management Geneva (SSBM Geneva). SSBM publishes a central application route and programme-specific entry requirements.

Applicants interested in an SSBM programme should:

1. Select the course and format using the [SSBM Geneva programme finder](https://www.ssbm.ch/course-search/).
2. Read the programme-specific entry requirements and the [SSBM admissions overview](https://www.ssbm.ch/admissions-overview/).
3. Prepare a CV or résumé, a recent photograph, a passport or accepted identity document, transcripts and certificates. Some programmes may request additional evidence.
4. Apply through the [SSBM Student Application System](https://apply.ssbm.ch/).
5. Contact [admissions@ssbm.ch](mailto:admissions@ssbm.ch) for application-status or programme-specific questions.

SSBM Geneva's published application guidance notes that campus programmes may have an application fee and specific intake dates. Online programme applications are subject to review by its Admissions Committee. Confirm current details directly with SSBM Geneva.

## Other education pathways

For programmes provided by other institutions, request the current entry criteria, prerequisite subjects, work-experience requirements, language requirements and application deadlines from the named provider.

## Suggested application steps

1. **Choose a pathway.** Review the [programme categories](/programmes/) and identify the qualification or subject area you are considering.
2. **Check eligibility.** Ask for the current entry criteria and any recognition-of-prior-learning arrangements.
3. **Request official documents.** Obtain the programme specification, awarding institution details, syllabus, delivery format, assessment rules and current fee schedule.
4. **Prepare your documents.** The provider may request identity and contact details, certificates and transcripts, a CV, work-experience evidence, references or language results. Use only the verified application channel.
5. **Submit the application.** Follow the application process and deadline stated by the responsible provider.
6. **Review the written decision.** Confirm any conditions, start date, payment instructions, cancellation terms and next steps before accepting an offer.

## What to ask before paying

Confirm the legal entity receiving payment, the exact qualification title, awarding institution, total cost, additional charges, refund conditions and the support available during study. See [fees and payment](/fees-and-payment/) and [accreditations and recognition](/about/recognition/).

## Important distinction

SUNKING GROUP may assist with information or a pathway enquiry. The institution named in the programme documents makes the admission decision and is responsible for the qualification it awards. Current programme availability and intake dates must be confirmed in writing.
