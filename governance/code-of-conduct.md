## Expected standards

Everyone participating in a SUNKING GROUP programme, event or service should:

- Treat learners, clients, colleagues, trainers and partners with dignity and respect.
- Communicate honestly and avoid misleading statements about qualifications, results or authority.
- Follow applicable laws, institutional regulations, safety instructions and contractual terms.
- Protect confidential information and personal data.
- Avoid bullying, harassment, discrimination, intimidation and retaliation.
- Declare relevant conflicts of interest.
- Use organisational resources, digital platforms and intellectual property responsibly.
- Give accurate information and correct errors promptly.
- Raise concerns through an appropriate channel rather than spreading unverified allegations.

## Learner and research conduct

Learners should submit their own work, cite sources, follow assessment rules and conduct research ethically. Any use of AI or external assistance must comply with the relevant institution's requirements.

## Client and consultant conduct

The parties to a consulting or training engagement should agree scope, decision rights, access to information, confidentiality and expected conduct before delivery begins.

## Reporting a concern

Concerns may be sent to [contact@sunkinggroup.org](mailto:contact@sunkinggroup.org). Where a third-party university or provider controls the programme or event, its own conduct and disciplinary procedures may also apply.
