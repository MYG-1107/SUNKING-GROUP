## Before making a payment

Request the complete written terms from the organisation that will receive your payment. The terms should explain:

- Which entity is contracting with you and receiving the payment.
- Whether application or registration fees are refundable.
- The cancellation deadline and any administrative charges.
- What happens if the provider cancels or changes the programme.
- Withdrawal, deferral and transfer options.
- Treatment of instalments, deposits and unpaid balances.
- The process for requesting a refund and the documents required.
- The expected processing steps and timeframe.

## Education programmes

Where an external institution delivers or awards a programme, its published refund, withdrawal and deferral rules may apply. Confirm whether SUNKING GROUP or the institution is responsible for receiving fees and processing any refund.

## Training and consulting services

For a training course or consulting engagement, the signed proposal or agreement should state cancellation notice, rescheduling, expenses, work completed and any non-refundable costs.

## Keep your records

Save the dated fee schedule, agreement, invoice, proof of payment and any cancellation request. If a term is unclear, obtain written clarification before paying.
