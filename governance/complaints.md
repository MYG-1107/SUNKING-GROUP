## How to raise a concern

If your concern relates to a SUNKING GROUP service, write to [contact@sunkinggroup.org](mailto:contact@sunkinggroup.org). Use the subject line “Complaint or grievance” and include:

- Your name and a safe way to contact you.
- The programme, service or engagement involved.
- Relevant dates, reference numbers and the people or teams involved, where known.
- A factual description of what happened and any steps already taken.
- Copies of relevant correspondence or documents, with unnecessary sensitive information removed.
- The outcome or remedy you are seeking.

## What happens next?

The concern should be directed to an appropriate person for review. The team may need to ask for clarification or supporting records. Do not assume that a complaint is formally registered until receipt has been acknowledged.

A response timeframe and escalation contact have not been confirmed for publication. The group should provide these details in its approved complaints procedure and confirm the process when acknowledging a complaint.

## Academic complaints and appeals

Where a university or other awarding body is responsible for admission, teaching, assessment, supervision or the award, its formal complaints and academic-appeal procedures may apply. Follow the deadlines and evidence requirements in that institution's current regulations.

## Privacy

Share only the information necessary to explain the concern. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or unrelated sensitive documents.
