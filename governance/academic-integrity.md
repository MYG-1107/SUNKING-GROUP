## Core expectations

Academic integrity supports trust in learning, assessment and research. Learners and contributors should:

- Submit work that represents their own contribution.
- Cite sources accurately and distinguish quotation, paraphrase and original analysis.
- Avoid plagiarism, fabrication, falsification, contract cheating and unauthorised collaboration.
- Obtain permission and ethics approval before collecting or using research data where required.
- Protect confidential information and personal data.
- Disclose relevant financial, personal or professional interests.
- Follow the rules for authorship, acknowledgements and publication.

## Generative AI and digital tools

The use of generative AI or other tools must follow the rules set by the relevant institution, journal or client. Where disclosure is required, learners and authors should describe the tool and the way it was used. Tools must not be used to invent sources, fabricate data, misrepresent authorship or submit unverified output as fact.

## Research with people or personal data

Projects involving participants, interviews, surveys, identifiable information or sensitive data may require ethics approval, informed consent, data minimisation and secure handling. Obtain the necessary approvals before starting data collection.

## Questions and reporting

Programme-specific rules, investigation procedures and possible outcomes are determined by the responsible awarding institution or contracting organisation. Request the official regulations if you are unsure how a rule applies.
