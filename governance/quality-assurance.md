## Purpose

SUNKING GROUP works across education-related activity, professional training, research development and consulting. Clear responsibilities and regular review are important in each area. The framework below describes the checks that should guide public information and service improvement.

## Quality principles

- **Accuracy:** programme, service, staff and partner information should be checked before publication.
- **Clear responsibility:** the institution, provider or team responsible for delivery, assessment, payment and awards should be identified.
- **Learner and client feedback:** feedback should be collected and reviewed with appropriate consent and privacy safeguards.
- **Defined outcomes:** proposals and programme specifications should explain their objectives, deliverables and assessment approach.
- **Continuous improvement:** feedback, incidents and review findings should inform proportionate improvements.
- **Accountability:** concerns should have a clear contact route and be handled by an appropriate person.

## Review cycle

A practical review cycle includes planning, delivery, feedback, review and corrective action. Records should identify the issue, evidence considered, decision, responsible person and follow-up date.

## Programme quality and awarding bodies

Where an external institution awards a qualification, its academic regulations and quality-assurance processes govern the formal programme. This page does not replace those rules. Learners should request the relevant handbook and current quality information directly from the awarding institution.

## Feedback

To raise a concern or suggest an improvement, contact [SUNKING GROUP](/contact/) and include the relevant service, dates and supporting information. Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the enquiry.
