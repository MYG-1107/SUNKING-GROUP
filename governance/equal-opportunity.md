## Our stated principles

SUNKING GROUP's published values refer to respect, inclusion, collaboration, gender neutrality and continuous development. These principles support an environment in which people are treated with dignity and can participate without unfair discrimination or harassment.

## Expected behaviour

People representing or participating in the group's activities should:

- Communicate respectfully and avoid discriminatory or humiliating behaviour.
- Make decisions using relevant, role-related and programme-related criteria.
- Respect different backgrounds, perspectives and personal circumstances.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, victimisation and retaliation.
- Consider reasonable accessibility needs and provide a clear route for requesting support.
- Protect personal information and handle concerns discreetly.

## Raising a concern

A person who experiences or witnesses behaviour that conflicts with these principles may contact [contact@sunkinggroup.org](mailto:contact@sunkinggroup.org). Include the activity, date, people involved where known and the support or action requested. Share only information necessary to explain the concern.

## Scope and review

Applicable legal duties and formal procedures can vary by country, provider and activity. Where a university, employer, venue or other organisation is responsible for the activity, its formal policy may also apply. This statement should be read with the relevant agreement and local rules.
