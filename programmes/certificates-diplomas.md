## What is the difference?

A **certificate** is often a shorter course focused on a defined topic or skill. A **diploma** generally covers a broader or more in-depth subject area. The names do not automatically indicate a particular academic level or recognition status; these depend on the awarding institution and its published framework.

Published SUNKING GROUP materials describe certificate categories in areas such as leadership, marketing, analytics and business, and diploma categories in areas such as strategic management, finance, marketing and digital business.

## Eligibility

Short courses may be open to beginners, students or working professionals, while advanced certificates and diplomas can require prior education, relevant experience or prerequisite knowledge. Applicants should confirm:

- Minimum education or work experience.
- Any required language level or technical background.
- Whether the programme is open to international applicants.
- Assessment, attendance and project requirements.
- Whether prior learning or credit transfer is considered.

## Indicative duration

Published category descriptions have referred to certificates of approximately **three to twelve months** and diplomas of approximately **one to two years**. These are planning ranges, not confirmed schedules for a current intake. The provider’s calendar and programme regulations take precedence.

## Outcomes and practical value

Depending on the syllabus, a certificate or diploma can help learners build subject knowledge, practise a defined skill, complete applied assignments and demonstrate continued professional learning. The value of a credential depends on its content, assessment, awarding body and relevance to the learner’s goals.

Do not assume that a short-course certificate is equivalent to a regulated professional licence, a full academic degree or a qualification accepted for credit by another institution.

## Enquiry and registration steps

1. Identify the subject, level and reason for studying.
2. Share your previous education and relevant experience.
3. Request the official syllabus, learning hours, duration and assessment method.
4. Confirm the awarding organisation, credential wording and independent recognition evidence.
5. Review fees, cancellation terms, delivery format and support arrangements.
6. Register through the provider’s verified application or enrolment channel.

The SUNKING GROUP team can help you identify the appropriate enquiry route. The programme provider remains responsible for admission, delivery and award decisions.
