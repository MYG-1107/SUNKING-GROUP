Programme overview

The online Master in Business Administration programme is described as supporting professionals who want to strengthen managerial skills, business knowledge and strategic leadership. The learning approach combines core concepts with practical application, case studies and projects related to real business situations.

The published overview describes an online format intended to help learners balance study with professional and personal commitments. Confirm the current mode of delivery, live session requirements, assessment schedule and support available to enrolled students.

Core business modules

  • Strategic management and leadership
  • Financial management and analysis
  • Marketing management and strategy
  • Operations and supply chain management
  • Human resource management
  • Business ethics and corporate governance

Advanced management topics

  • International business and global strategy
  • Innovation and entrepreneurship
  • Business analytics and decision-making
  • Change management and organisational development
  • Project management
  • Risk management and compliance

Specialisation areas described

  • Finance and investment management
  • Marketing and brand management
  • Human resources and organisational behaviour
  • Operations and technology management
  • International business and trade
  • Entrepreneurship and innovation

Learning outcomes

Capabilities the programme aims to develop

Outcomes depend on the final syllabus, assessment methods and the learner's own experience and participation.

Strategic leadership

Consider business direction, strategic choices and organisational priorities.

Business analysis

Evaluate performance, identify opportunities and work through complex business problems.

Global perspective

Understand international business dynamics, cultural differences and market trends.

Team management

Lead diverse teams, support change and encourage high-performance working.

Innovation and creativity

Explore new business models, services and approaches to value creation.

Professional communication

Present ideas, negotiate and build constructive stakeholder relationships.

Admissions and careers

What applicants should review

Academic requirements

Published summaries refer to a bachelor's degree, a minimum GPA or equivalent, official transcripts and English-language evidence where applicable. Confirm current requirements with the institution.

Professional experience

The published page refers to at least two years of professional experience and demonstrated leadership potential. The exact requirements may change.

Application materials

Potential materials include an application form, statement of purpose, recommendations and a CV. Follow the institution's current checklist.

Possible career directions

Potential roles include Chief Executive Officer, Chief Operating Officer, general manager, operations director, financial manager, investment analyst, marketing or brand manager, sales manager, HR director, talent manager, technology manager, supply-chain leader, entrepreneur, consultant or business adviser, depending on experience.

Application process

Typical steps to confirm

The current process and exact steps are controlled by the awarding institution; use its latest application guidance.

  1. Submit the application using the institution’s current application route.
  2. Provide required records, transcripts, references and supporting documents.
  3. Complete an interview or further assessment if requested.
  4. Review the formal admission decision, fees, terms and award details.
  5. Complete enrolment requirements and confirm the start date.
Before committing, request the official curriculum, entry criteria, total programme cost, refund policy, assessment rules, completion requirements and documentation of recognition or accreditation where relevant.
