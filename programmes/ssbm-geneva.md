## Our partnership with SSBM Geneva

SUNKING GROUP is a **Business Partner of the Swiss School of Business and Management Geneva (SSBM Geneva)**. Through this relationship, we help prospective learners explore programme options and understand the information they should review before applying.

SSBM Geneva is responsible for its own admissions decisions, academic requirements, programme delivery and qualifications it awards. Applications are submitted through SSBM Geneva's official Student Application System (SAS). A conversation with SUNKING GROUP is an information enquiry; it is not an offer of admission or a guarantee that an applicant meets the entry requirements.

- [Visit SSBM Geneva's official website](https://www.ssbm.ch/)
- [Browse the official programme finder](https://www.ssbm.ch/course-search/)
- [Read SSBM Geneva's admissions overview](https://www.ssbm.ch/admissions-overview/)
- [View SSBM Geneva's application process](https://www.ssbm.ch/apply-to-ssbm/)

## Programme categories

SSBM Geneva's published catalogue includes study options across the following areas. The precise course titles, awarding arrangements, availability and delivery format should always be checked on the individual programme page.

### Bachelor's programmes

Undergraduate options include business administration and related areas. The online catalogue also lists subject routes such as international management, business IT, information technology, sports management and artificial intelligence. Entry decisions depend on the applicant's prior education and the requirements for the selected programme.

### Master's programmes

Master's-level options include management and specialist subjects. The current online catalogue includes management and artificial intelligence / strategic leadership pathways, alongside other specialist master's routes. Check the exact degree title, curriculum and admission criteria before applying.

### MBA programmes

MBA options are intended for learners looking to deepen business and management knowledge. The live catalogue includes general and specialist areas such as global management, executive management, finance, data science, digital marketing, artificial intelligence, hospitality, sports management and international relations. Not every specialisation is necessarily available in every format or intake.

### DBA and doctorate programmes

Doctoral options include applied business research routes. The SSBM catalogue lists DBA categories such as global and executive DBA, dissertation track, top-up DBA and project management DBA. Applicants should review the research expectations, supervisor arrangements, milestones, examination requirements and award details for the specific programme.

### Other learning options

The official catalogue also includes a Doctor of Education (Ed.D.), postdoctoral research opportunities, certificate and diploma programmes, and executive education. These options have different purposes and entry requirements; they should not be treated as equivalent to a bachelor's, master's or doctoral degree.

## Study formats

SSBM Geneva lists programmes in more than one format. Availability depends on the specific course:

- **Geneva campus:** on-site study based on the course's published schedule and attendance requirements.
- **Online:** study through the provider's online learning environment, with the requirements and pace described on the programme page.
- **Blended:** a combination of online learning and scheduled in-person elements.

Do not assume that every programme is available in all three formats. Check campus location, attendance expectations, start dates, time zone, assessment method and any visa or travel implications before choosing.

## Admission requirements: what applicants should prepare

SSBM Geneva publishes programme-specific entry rules. The following is a guide to the types of requirements shown on its admissions pages; it is not an eligibility assessment.

| Programme level | Published entry information to review |
| --- | --- |
| Bachelor | An upper-secondary qualification such as the Swiss Baccalaureate, High School Diploma, International Baccalaureate or equivalent. SSBM also lists English-language evidence for applicants who are not from a majority English-speaking country, with TOEFL iBT 90, IELTS 6.5 or an accepted equivalent referenced on its admissions page. |
| Master | An undergraduate degree from a recognised university or equivalent, transcripts, CV, identity document, photograph and evidence of English proficiency where required. Additional management courses or recognition-of-prior-learning conditions may apply in some cases. |
| MBA | An undergraduate degree from a recognised university or equivalent, transcripts, CV, identity document, photograph and English-language evidence where required. Executive MBA applicants may have additional postgraduate education and work-experience requirements. |
| DBA / Doctorate | SSBM lists routes based on an MBA or equivalent, or a relevant master's degree combined with professional experience. The required experience depends on the prior qualification and the programme. |

The awarding institution decides whether prior qualifications and experience meet its requirements. Request a formal assessment from SSBM Geneva if your qualification is from a different country, uses a different credit system or is not in a related subject.

## Application process

SSBM Geneva directs applicants to its central Student Application System. A typical preparation sequence is:

1. Choose the exact programme and confirm whether it is online, campus-based or blended.
2. Read the current programme page and admissions criteria.
3. Prepare your CV or résumé, a recent photograph, passport or other accepted identity document, transcripts and certificates. Check whether the programme asks for additional documents.
4. Submit the application using SSBM Geneva's official [Student Application System](https://apply.ssbm.ch/).
5. Follow up directly with the SSBM admissions team about missing documents, an interview or an admission decision.
6. Review the official offer, fee schedule, payment instructions, refund rules and enrolment terms before paying.

**SSBM Geneva's admissions contact:** [admissions@ssbm.ch](mailto:admissions@ssbm.ch).

The school's application information states that campus programmes may have an application fee and specific intake deadlines. Online programme entry is subject to review by the Admissions Committee. Always confirm the current process for the programme you select.

## Tuition fees and scholarships

Fees vary by programme and delivery format. SSBM Geneva publishes its own [tuition-fee schedule](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/) and information about payment options and possible discounts. Amounts, currencies, additional fees and eligibility conditions may change. Use the live fee page and request a written fee breakdown from SSBM before making a payment.

SUNKING GROUP does not set SSBM tuition fees and cannot promise admission, a scholarship, a discount or a particular payment arrangement. Any financial assistance must be confirmed by the institution in writing.

## What to check before you enrol

Before accepting an offer, make sure you can answer these questions:

- What is the exact qualification title and which institution awards it?
- Is this the correct programme for my previous education and career plans?
- What are the full fees, currency, instalment terms and additional charges?
- Which study format is available for my intake, and are there any compulsory campus visits?
- What are the assessment, attendance, research and completion requirements?
- What are the cancellation, deferral and refund conditions?
- Which institution handles student records, academic support and formal complaints?

Keep the programme specification, formal offer, fee schedule and any written clarification from the provider.
