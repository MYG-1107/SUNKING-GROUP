## Who may be eligible?

Most master’s programmes require a recognised bachelor’s degree or an equivalent qualification. Some programmes accept applicants from a range of academic disciplines, while others require relevant subject preparation, professional experience or prerequisite modules.

Depending on the provider, applicants may be asked for academic transcripts, a CV, a statement of purpose, references, proof of English proficiency or an interview. A programme may also specify minimum grades, work experience or a professional portfolio. Only the awarding institution can confirm whether an applicant meets its current entry criteria.

## Expected duration and format

Many full-time master’s programmes are planned over **one to two years**. Part-time, online, accelerated and credit-transfer routes may follow different calendars. The existing SUNKING GROUP programme catalogue also describes MBA categories with an indicative one-to-two-year duration; confirm the current intake and study plan directly with the named institution.

Ask whether the programme is fully online, blended or campus-based; whether live attendance is compulsory; how examinations and projects are assessed; and whether any residency or practical component is required.

## Learning outcomes

A postgraduate programme may help learners to:

- Build advanced knowledge in a defined discipline or management area.
- Apply frameworks and evidence to complex professional problems.
- Evaluate information, research and business decisions critically.
- Communicate recommendations to specialist and non-specialist audiences.
- Develop leadership, project or specialist capability relevant to the course.

The award itself does not guarantee career progression, salary changes or professional recognition. Verify any regulated or licensed career pathway with the relevant professional body.

## Application and registration steps

1. Choose a subject area and define why postgraduate study is relevant to your goals.
2. Check whether your previous degree and experience meet the official entry requirements.
3. Request the current prospectus, syllabus, intake dates, delivery details and full fee schedule.
4. Verify the awarding institution, exact award title, credit structure and recognition independently.
5. Submit the application and supporting documents using the institution’s official process.
6. Read the offer, payment schedule, refund policy and programme regulations before accepting.
