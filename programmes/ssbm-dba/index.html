What is a DBA?

A Doctor of Business Administration is a professional doctorate that applies research to management and organisational practice. A candidate normally investigates a defined question, works with relevant academic literature and research methods, analyses evidence and presents a substantial research output for examination.

DBA programmes are generally suited to experienced professionals who want to develop their ability to examine complex business problems systematically. The topic may relate to strategy, leadership, innovation, people management, finance, technology or another area approved by the institution.

DBA routes listed by SSBM Geneva

SSBM Geneva's public catalogue lists several doctoral routes, including DBA Global, Executive DBA, DBA Dissertation Track, Top-Up DBA and a DBA programme in Project Management. It also lists dual-degree or collaborative doctoral options. These routes are not interchangeable; entry criteria, curriculum, partner institutions, delivery and award details can differ.

Use the official SSBM programme finder to locate the current course page, then request the full programme specification.

What doctoral research involves

  • Defining a research problem and a focused question.
  • Reviewing relevant academic and professional literature.
  • Selecting an appropriate research design and method.
  • Considering research ethics and the responsible treatment of data.
  • Collecting and analysing evidence in line with the approved research plan.
  • Presenting findings, limitations and practical implications.
  • Completing the institution's required assessment and examination process.

Who may consider this route?

Applicants should have the academic background and experience required by the specific programme. SSBM Geneva's admissions overview describes doctorate entry routes based on an MBA or equivalent from a recognised higher education institution, or a relevant master's qualification combined with professional experience. The exact requirement depends on the applicant's prior field and programme.

Questions to ask the admissions team

Which DBA route matches my background?

Ask SSBM Geneva to review your highest qualification, subject area and professional experience against the entry criteria for the specific DBA route.

Who will supervise my research?

Request details about supervisor allocation, contact frequency, research milestones, feedback and any formal supervisory requirements.

What are the research and examination requirements?

Ask for the current research handbook, ethics process, dissertation expectations, examination method, possible viva and completion rules.

What is the full cost and how long can I take?

Ask for a written fee schedule, expected duration, maximum registration period, extension charges and refund or withdrawal terms.
