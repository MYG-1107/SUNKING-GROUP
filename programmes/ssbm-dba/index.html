Doctor of Business Administration (DBA)

A professional doctorate category focused on advanced business practice, research and contributions to business knowledge.

Programme overview

The Doctor of Business Administration programme is described as a doctoral-level pathway for experienced professionals and executives who want to advance their practice and make contributions to business theory and application. It combines academic study with practical problem-solving and applied research.

Published information describes flexible study options, including campus-based study in Geneva and online delivery. The current study format, campus attendance, course structure and assessment requirements should be confirmed directly with the institution.

Curriculum areas

  • Research methodology: advanced methods and techniques for business research.
  • Strategic leadership: leadership theories and strategic management principles.
  • Innovation management: leading innovation and transformation in complex organisations.
  • Global business: international business strategy and cross-cultural management.
  • Financial strategy: advanced decision-making and corporate finance.
  • Dissertation: an original research project contributing to business knowledge.

Study formats described

Geneva campus

  • Campus-based learning and residencies, subject to current arrangements.
  • Opportunities for direct faculty interaction and peer collaboration.
  • Potential engagement with the Geneva business ecosystem.

Online learning

  • Remote delivery through an online learning environment.
  • Flexible scheduling suited to working professionals.
  • Virtual classroom and online collaboration arrangements, as specified by the provider.

Admissions and outcomes

What candidates should confirm

Entry requirements

Published summaries refer to a master's degree or equivalent, management experience, professional references, a statement of purpose, English proficiency and an interview where required. Ask for the official current admission criteria.

Potential professional directions

Graduates may pursue senior leadership, management consulting, board-level, academic research or industry-specialist roles, depending on their background and the qualification's recognition.

Before enrolling

Request the programme regulations, official award title, recognition documentation, exact fees, refund terms, duration and delivery information in writing.
