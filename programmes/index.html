Study format

Online, campus or blended?

The delivery format is programme-specific. Do not assume that every programme is available in every mode.

Online

Remote learning. Check live-session requirements, access to learning materials, assessment method and expected study schedule.

Campus-based

In-person study at the named location. Confirm attendance dates, visa considerations, accommodation and any campus application fee.

Blended

A combination of online study and in-person activity. Ask which elements require travel and whether attendance is compulsory.
