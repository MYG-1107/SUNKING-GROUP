## Who may be eligible?

Undergraduate degree routes are generally designed for learners who have completed upper-secondary education (such as Class 12 or an equivalent qualification). The accepted board, subject combination, minimum marks, language requirements and any entrance assessment depend on the institution and programme.

Applicants should be ready to provide their school-leaving certificate, mark sheets, identity documents and any additional records requested by the awarding institution. International applicants may need credential equivalency or translated documents.

## Expected duration and study format

A bachelor’s degree is commonly planned over **three to four academic years**, but the actual duration can vary by country, discipline, credit structure and provider. Part-time study, transfer credit, internships or professional placements can affect the timeline.

Available materials describe a mix of online and campus-based categories. Do not assume that every subject or intake is offered in both formats. Confirm attendance, teaching hours, examination arrangements, practical requirements and technology access before registering.

## Learning outcomes

Depending on the chosen subject and syllabus, undergraduate study can help learners to:

- Build foundational knowledge in a chosen academic or professional area.
- Develop written communication, research, presentation and analytical skills.
- Work collaboratively and approach problems systematically.
- Understand professional ethics and responsible decision-making.
- Prepare for further study or entry-level opportunities relevant to the discipline.

A qualification does not guarantee employment, promotion or professional licensure. Career outcomes depend on the award, the learner’s skills and experience, labour-market conditions and any applicable regulatory requirements.

## How to enquire or register

1. **Tell us your background:** share your Class 12 or equivalent qualification, subjects and year of completion.
2. **Identify your goal:** explain the subject area, career interest and preferred study mode.
3. **Review current options:** ask for the exact institution, programme title, intake status and official course information.
4. **Check the evidence:** obtain written confirmation of eligibility, total fees, refund terms, assessment rules, awarding body and recognition status.
5. **Apply through the official route:** submit documents and payment only through the verified application channel provided by the institution.

## Questions to ask before paying

Request the current prospectus, programme regulations, complete fee schedule, refund and cancellation policy, academic calendar, contact details for the awarding institution and independent evidence of any accreditation or recognition claim. SUNKING GROUP can help route an enquiry; the final admission decision rests with the institution.
