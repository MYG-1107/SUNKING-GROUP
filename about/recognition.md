## What should be verified?

The words “accredited”, “recognised”, “approved” and “international” can refer to different things. Before enrolling, request the exact qualification title and check:

1. The legal name of the awarding institution.
2. The institution's authority to award that qualification in its home jurisdiction.
3. The programme's approval or accreditation status, where relevant.
4. Whether the recognition applies to the exact programme, delivery mode and intake.
5. Whether the qualification meets the requirements of the regulator, employer or professional body for your intended use.

## Different roles

SUNKING GROUP may describe, support, advise on or facilitate an education pathway. The university or other named awarding institution is responsible for the qualification it awards and its published academic regulations. These roles must be stated clearly in the programme documents.

## Independent verification

Use the relevant government regulator, recognised quality-assurance agency, awarding institution or professional body to verify a claim. Do not rely only on a brochure, agent statement, third-party directory or general institutional marketing page.

No accreditation certificate, registration number or programme approval record has been supplied here for independent publication. Request current evidence for the specific qualification you are considering.
