## Markets referenced in public materials

The group's public-facing organisational description refers to work or activity connected with:

- India
- The United Kingdom
- The United States
- The United Arab Emirates
- Europe

These broad references describe markets and regions mentioned in existing materials. They should not be read as a verified list of incorporated entities, permanent offices, campuses, branches or country representatives.

## Office and regional contact information

A country-wise directory with current street addresses, telephone numbers, office hours and named representatives has not been provided for publication. To avoid misleading visitors, this page does not publish unverified addresses or imply that a local office is available in every listed market.

For a regional enquiry, please include your country, preferred contact method and the service you are interested in. The group can then confirm the appropriate point of contact and clarify whether the requested service is available in that location.

## Working across borders

Before enrolling in an international programme or engaging a cross-border service, ask for written confirmation of the contracting entity, delivery location, applicable currency, taxes or additional charges, payment beneficiary, data-handling arrangements and the process for complaints or cancellations.
