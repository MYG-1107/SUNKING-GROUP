## Public-facing identity

SUNKING GROUP is the name used across this website to present management education, research-related activity, professional training, leadership development and consulting services.

A group or trading name is not necessarily the same as the legal entity that signs a contract, invoices a client, collects a payment or carries a regulatory responsibility. The responsible legal entity should be stated in the relevant proposal, enrolment agreement, invoice or service contract.

## Details to verify

Before entering an agreement or making a payment, request and independently check the following information:

- Full registered legal name and legal structure.
- Country and jurisdiction of registration.
- Registration or company number, where applicable.
- Registered address and the relevant service or delivery address.
- Name of the entity responsible for the programme, training or consulting engagement.
- Correct payment beneficiary and official payment instructions.
- Contact details for complaints, privacy requests and contractual notices.

## Programme and qualification responsibility

For education pathways, also confirm the institution that admits the learner, delivers the teaching, assesses the work and awards the qualification. These responsibilities may sit with different organisations. They should not be inferred from the SUNKING GROUP brand name or from a general reference to an institutional relationship.

## Transparency

Registration numbers, legal addresses and other entity details are not displayed here because verified source documents have not been supplied for publication. Please request the applicable details directly and review the written documents before paying or enrolling.
