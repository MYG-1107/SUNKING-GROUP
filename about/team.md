## Leadership

The website currently publishes a profile of [Professor (Dr.) Chaitanya Niphadkar](/leadership/), Founder and President of SUNKING GROUP. Please refer to that page for the biography and external references currently available.

## Faculty, coordinators and consultants

A complete public directory of faculty members, academic coordinators, consultants, regional representatives and support staff has not yet been supplied for publication. We will not add names, photographs, qualifications or professional memberships without confirmation and permission to publish.

When profiles are added, each should explain:

- The person's current role and area of responsibility.
- Relevant qualifications and professional experience.
- Subject specialisations and the work they undertake.
- Whether the person is a permanent employee, visiting faculty member, adviser, contractor or external specialist.
- A professional reference or profile link, where available and approved.

## Academic and programme responsibility

A person's profile should not be taken as proof that they are the academic lead or awarding authority for a particular qualification. The programme documentation should identify the institution responsible for admission, teaching, assessment and certification.

## Enquiries

For questions about faculty availability, supervision, trainers or consultants, contact the group with the subject area and the programme or service you are considering.
