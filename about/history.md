## Our background

SUNKING GROUP presents its work across management education, research-oriented learning, leadership development, professional training and business consulting. Its public-facing materials describe activity connected with India, the United Kingdom, the United States, the United Arab Emirates and Europe.

A complete, independently verifiable timeline—including the founding date, legal milestones, major programme launches and dated organisational achievements—has not yet been published on this website. We will only add milestones when the date, description and supporting evidence have been checked.

## How we will document milestones

A useful organisational timeline should identify:

- The date and nature of each event.
- The legal entity or operating unit involved.
- Whether the milestone relates to a programme, partnership, project, publication or organisational development.
- Supporting evidence, such as a public filing, official announcement, signed agreement or publication record.
- Any limitations on what the milestone demonstrates.

## Request an organisational profile

Institutions, prospective learners and business clients may request the latest organisational profile and ask for clarification about the entity responsible for a particular service or agreement. Please use the [contact page](/contact/) and identify the information you need.
