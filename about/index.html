About the organisation

Education, people development and consulting

SUNKING GROUP is presented as an international organisation working with people and organisations across India, the United Kingdom, the United States, the United Arab Emirates and Europe. Its work brings together education, consultancy and professional development across different markets.

Public-facing organisational materials describe education operations in India, consulting activity in the United Arab Emirates, and work with organisations in the United States, United Kingdom and Europe. The group's education operations focus on flexible learning, management education and research. Its published approach emphasises experiential learning and programmes intended to help learners strengthen knowledge and skills for the future of business. Its consulting and training activities cover business, human resources, management, media, technology education and personal competency development.

The group's organisational culture highlights respect for learners, employees and clients, work-life balance, gender neutrality, collaboration and continuous development. Programme availability, delivery, awarding arrangements and recognition vary and should be confirmed for each specific programme before an applicant makes a decision.

Our mission

“To democratize quality management education through inclusive, flexible, and globally connected programs that empower professionals and scholars to excel, innovate, and lead in a diverse world.”

Our vision

“To transform lives worldwide with flexible, research-rich management programs that develop forward-thinking leaders equipped to drive global change.”

Our areas of work

  • Management education, including certificate and diploma categories and postgraduate and doctoral pathways, subject to provider requirements.
  • Research-oriented learning and academic development, including doctoral and post-doctoral research support.
  • Leadership and team development training.
  • Business, human resources and management consultancy.
  • Technology education research and development, administrative training, media consultancy and related arts training.

We aim to be a connected destination for education, professional training and business consulting enquiries. Contact the group to discuss your current needs and confirm available services.
