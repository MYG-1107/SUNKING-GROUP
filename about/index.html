What SUNKING GROUP does

We work with people who want to build their knowledge and professional capabilities, and with organisations that want to strengthen their management, people practices and learning. The work covers education guidance, leadership and team development, research-related support and business consulting.

For prospective learners, a good starting point is understanding the qualification, the institution responsible for it, the entry requirements and the complete cost. For organisations, it is understanding the issue to be addressed, the people involved and the outcomes that can reasonably be agreed.

Our role varies by service. In education, SUNKING GROUP can provide information and guidance about published programme opportunities. The institution named in the programme documents is responsible for admissions, academic delivery, assessment and the qualification it awards. In training and consulting, the scope and responsibilities are agreed with the client before work begins.

Our mission

To democratise quality management education through inclusive, flexible and globally connected programmes that empower professionals and scholars to excel, innovate and lead in a diverse world.

Our vision

To contribute to a world in which people have access to learning, research and practical development that helps them lead responsibly and create positive change.

Integrity

Communicating clearly and being honest about responsibilities, qualifications and outcomes.

Excellence

Taking care with the quality of information, learning and professional work.

Innovation

Being open to new ideas, thoughtful experimentation and continuous improvement.

Collaboration

Working respectfully with learners, colleagues, institutions and organisations.

Lifelong learning

Encouraging curiosity, reflection and ongoing professional development.

Respect and inclusion

Valuing people, different perspectives and fair opportunities to participate.

Our areas of work

Education and research

We help prospective learners explore programme pathways, understand application information and identify the documents they should request from the provider. We also publish guidance on research development and responsible academic practice.

Leadership and professional development

Training topics include leadership, communication, coaching, teamwork, change management and workplace effectiveness. A programme is designed around the participants and organisation rather than assuming that one workshop suits every team.

Business consulting

Consulting enquiries may involve business management, people and HR, organisational development, education or research-related needs. The first step is to understand the challenge and decide whether a defined engagement is appropriate.

Our partnership with SSBM Geneva

SUNKING GROUP is a Business Partner of the Swiss School of Business and Management Geneva (SSBM Geneva). Prospective learners can explore SSBM's published Bachelor, Master, MBA, DBA / doctorate and other programme options through our SSBM Geneva programmes and admissions page. SSBM Geneva controls its own admissions process, academic requirements and awards.

Who we work with

Our enquiries come from students, graduates, working professionals, managers, small and medium-sized businesses, institutions and organisations looking for a learning or advisory conversation. The right next step depends on the person's goals and the provider or service involved.
