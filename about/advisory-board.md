## Advisory appointments

No named advisory board directory has been provided for publication on this website. We will not publish assumed members, affiliations or credentials.

When verified appointments are confirmed, the directory should identify each adviser, the date or period of appointment, relevant expertise, current professional affiliation and the scope of the advisory role.

## What an advisory role means

An adviser may provide strategic, academic or industry guidance. An advisory appointment does not, by itself, mean that the adviser is an employee, programme examiner, supervisor, awarding authority, regulator or representative of another institution.

## Independence and disclosure

Any relevant relationship or conflict of interest should be disclosed clearly. Where an adviser is also affiliated with an external university, company or professional body, that relationship should not be presented as an endorsement unless the organisation has formally authorised the claim.

For enquiries about a specific programme, request the official details of the institution responsible for delivery and award.
