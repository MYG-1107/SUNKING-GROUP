## Areas of research development

Research-related enquiries may involve choosing a topic, refining a research question, understanding research design, reviewing literature, selecting methods, planning data collection, interpreting findings, structuring a dissertation or preparing a manuscript.

Support may also cover research integrity, citation practice, responsible authorship, publication ethics and the responsible use of research tools.

## Doctoral supervision and award responsibility

A doctoral candidate must confirm who is formally authorised to supervise, examine and award the qualification. Informal advice or research-development support is not the same as official supervision or academic approval.

Before proceeding, request written confirmation of:

- The awarding institution and exact qualification.
- The named supervisor or the process used to appoint one.
- Research proposal and progression requirements.
- Ethics approval and data-protection requirements.
- Examination, viva and completion rules.
- Any fees for supervision, examination or publication support.

## Responsible research

The researcher's institution and relevant ethics committee determine the rules that apply to a project. Do not collect personal data, recruit participants or begin a regulated study until the required approvals and safeguards are in place.

## No guarantee of publication or award

Research guidance cannot guarantee acceptance by a journal, a positive examination outcome or award of a degree. The learner remains responsible for original work, accurate reporting and compliance with the relevant institution's regulations.
