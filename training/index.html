Practical methods

Learning through participation

Training methods described in the group's published materials focus on practice, discussion and application after the session.

  • Case studiesExplore real-life scenarios and consider alternative responses.
  • Role-play and simulationPractise conversations, decisions and leadership situations in a safe setting.
  • Group discussions and exercisesCompare perspectives, test assumptions and learn from colleagues.
  • Action planningTranslate session insights into specific implementation steps.
  • Coaching and feedbackUse reflection and feedback to support improvement over time.
