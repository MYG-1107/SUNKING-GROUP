## Training for organisational needs

A corporate training enquiry begins with the capability or performance challenge an organisation wants to address. The programme should be designed around the audience, work context, level of experience and outcomes that matter to the organisation.

## Areas to explore

- **Leadership development:** leadership practice, decision-making, coaching, change and team leadership.
- **Communication at work:** feedback, workplace communication, collaboration and conflict management.
- **Team performance:** role clarity, trust, coordination and continuous improvement.
- **Professional development:** management capabilities, problem-solving, customer orientation and workplace effectiveness.
- **Learning and change:** supporting people through process, technology and organisational changes.

See the existing [leadership development](/training/leadership-development/), [communication and workplace](/training/communication-workplace/) and [team performance](/training/team-performance-change/) pages for further context.

## How a programme is scoped

The initial discussion should identify the sponsor, participant profile, learning objectives, expected group size, delivery mode, language, location, timing and any accessibility requirements. A proposal should then state the learning design, facilitator responsibilities, deliverables, evaluation method, costs and cancellation terms.

## Delivery and evaluation

Delivery may be discussed as an in-person workshop, virtual session, blended programme or a series of learning activities. The final format, trainer, duration and schedule must be confirmed in writing. Evaluation can include participant feedback and agreed learning checks; it should not promise business results that have not been measured.

No public price list or fixed calendar is currently published. Contact the team to request a programme-specific proposal.
