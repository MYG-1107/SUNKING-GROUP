## Who should consider this programme?

The training can be relevant to intact teams, project groups, cross-functional teams, new managers, supervisors and employees working through organisational change. It is most useful when a sponsor can explain the reason for the programme and support participants in applying the learning afterwards.

A preliminary discussion should establish whether the need is primarily team-building, role clarity, communication, conflict management, change readiness or performance conversations.

## Duration and format

A focused team workshop may be designed for **one or two days**. More complex work can be delivered as a sequence of sessions over **four to eight weeks**, with activities between sessions. Change-management learning may be combined with leadership development or coaching where agreed.

These are indicative planning windows. The final time commitment depends on the group size, objectives, delivery method and availability of participants.

## Learning outcomes and activities

Possible content includes team roles, trust, shared goals, constructive conflict, decision-making, feedback, accountability, remote and hybrid collaboration, change communication and stakeholder engagement.

Participants may work towards the ability to:

- Clarify responsibilities, dependencies and decision rights.
- Agree shared goals and practical working norms.
- Raise concerns and address conflict constructively.
- Communicate the reason for change and listen to stakeholder feedback.
- Identify risks to implementation and agree appropriate actions.
- Use review meetings and feedback to improve team practice.

Facilitated activities can include team mapping, scenarios, simulations, retrospectives and action planning. Any evaluation measures should be selected with the client and should use an agreed baseline.

## Enquiry steps

1. Identify the sponsor, team size and work context.
2. Describe the change, coordination issue or capability gap.
3. Agree what success should look like and how it could be observed.
4. Confirm the session plan, facilitator, timeframe, fees and cancellation terms.
5. Arrange participant registration and any preparation material.
6. Review the agreed outcomes and next steps after the session.
