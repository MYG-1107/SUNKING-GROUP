## Current public schedule

No dated public training calendar has been supplied for publication at this time. We will not display assumed dates, trainer names, prices, registration deadlines or available places.

## What a confirmed listing should include

When a training session is approved for publication, its listing should state:

- Session title, purpose and learning outcomes.
- Date, local time and time zone.
- Venue or online delivery platform.
- Intended audience and any prerequisites.
- Trainer or facilitator profile.
- Duration, language and accessibility information.
- Fee, taxes and what the fee includes.
- Registration process, closing date and cancellation terms.
- Whether a certificate is offered and which organisation issues it.

## Private and customised sessions

Organisations can enquire about an in-house workshop or a customised programme. The date, format, participant capacity, facilitator, learning materials and commercial terms are agreed as part of the proposal.

A request for information is not a booking or confirmed registration. The team will need to confirm availability and provide written details before a session can be considered scheduled.
