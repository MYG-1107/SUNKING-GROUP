## Start with the operating context

Industry-focused support should reflect an organisation's customers, regulatory environment, operating model, workforce, technology and strategic priorities. The solution should be based on discovery rather than a generic package.

## Areas that may be relevant

- **Business and management:** planning, organisational structure, process improvement and management capability.
- **People and human resources:** workforce development, leadership, employee experience and change.
- **Education and research:** programme design discussions, research development and institutional capability.
- **Technology education:** learning needs connected with digital transformation and emerging technologies.
- **Professional development:** communication, teamwork, problem-solving and workplace capability.

These are areas for discussion, not a published list of completed sector projects or guaranteed specialist credentials. The relevant expertise and delivery team should be confirmed for each proposal.

## How a suitable engagement is determined

The discovery stage should document the sector, organisation size, location, intended beneficiaries, issue, constraints, required expertise and expected deliverables. The proposal should then identify the people responsible and any specialist partner or supplier involved.

No client names, case studies or quantified sector outcomes are published here without documented permission and supporting evidence. For an overview of the engagement stages, see the [consultancy process](/consulting/process/).
