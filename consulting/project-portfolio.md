## A transparent portfolio

A project case study should identify the challenge, scope, delivery period, team role, approach, deliverables and results. Where a client permits publication, the case study should also state the client's name and the basis for any reported figures.

If confidentiality prevents naming a client, the case study should explain that it is anonymised and avoid details that could identify the organisation indirectly.

## Evidence expected in a case study

- Client permission and an accurate description of the relationship.
- The context and problem addressed.
- The work SUNKING GROUP actually performed.
- The period and scope of the engagement.
- Deliverables and methods used.
- Measured outcomes, including the measurement method and limitations.
- Any external supplier or partner involved.

## Request relevant experience

The team can be asked to describe relevant capabilities during a discovery discussion. Any examples shared should make clear whether they are completed projects, illustrative methods or proposed approaches. No client name, testimonial, award or quantified result should be inferred from a general service description.
