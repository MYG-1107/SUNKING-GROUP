A practical approach

Start with the need, then agree the scope.

Effective advisory work starts with clear objectives and an understanding of the organisation's situation, constraints and desired outcomes.

Before an engagement

  • Define the business or capability challenge.
  • Agree scope, deliverables, timeframe and roles.
  • Confirm fees, confidentiality and any data-handling needs.
  • Decide how progress and outcomes will be reviewed.
