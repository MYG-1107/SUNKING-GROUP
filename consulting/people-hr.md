## Who may benefit?

This service area can be relevant to business owners, HR teams, managers and organisations reviewing workforce practices or investing in people development. The initial discussion should identify the jurisdiction, sector, workforce size and whether specialist legal or regulated advice is needed.

## Possible advisory topics

Depending on the agreed scope, discussions may cover:

- Workforce planning and capability needs.
- Role clarity, responsibilities and team structure.
- Learning and development priorities.
- Leadership, engagement and feedback practices.
- Performance-management processes and manager capability.
- Change communication and organisational development.
- People-related policies and process reviews, with legal review where required.

The engagement should distinguish between management guidance, implementation support and advice that must be provided by a qualified employment-law or compliance professional.

## Indicative duration and outputs

A defined diagnostic or discovery exercise may take **one to two weeks**. A focused review with stakeholder discussions and recommendations may take **three to six weeks**. A longer people-development or change programme should be planned in stages.

Possible agreed outputs include a current-state summary, capability priorities, process observations, recommendations, a manager-development outline or an action roadmap. No improvement in retention, engagement or productivity is guaranteed; such measures need an agreed baseline and reliable follow-up data.

## How an organisation can enquire

1. Describe the business, sector, workforce and location.
2. Identify the people challenge and why it matters now.
3. Confirm the scope, stakeholders, information needed and any sensitive data.
4. Agree confidentiality, data handling, deliverables, fees and timeline in writing.
5. Obtain specialist legal or regulatory review where required.
6. Review the recommendations and decide which actions the organisation will own.
