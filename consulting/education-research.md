## Areas of interest

The group’s published service categories include technology education research and development, academic development and related professional learning. Depending on expertise and scope, an enquiry may concern:

- Reviewing an education or training concept.
- Planning a learning programme and its intended outcomes.
- Considering the use of digital tools in teaching and learning.
- Developing a research question and preliminary project plan.
- Reviewing research-methods choices and academic writing structure.
- Planning dissemination or a publication strategy consistent with journal requirements.

Any support must preserve the learner’s or researcher’s independent contribution. It must not involve fabricated data, purchased authorship, plagiarism, manipulated citations or guarantees of publication.

## Who should enquire?

Potential enquirers include education providers, academic teams, researchers, professional bodies and organisations designing learning initiatives. The initial discussion should identify the project owner, intended learners or research participants, institutional requirements, available resources and the decision that needs to be made.

## Indicative timeline and deliverables

A first-stage planning discussion and written scope may take **one to two weeks**. A defined learning-design or research-planning assignment may take **three to six weeks**, depending on approvals, stakeholder input and the material available.

Possible deliverables, where agreed, include a project brief, learning-outcome map, high-level delivery plan, research-question refinement notes, methods discussion or publication-readiness checklist. These are advisory outputs, not academic approval or a promise of acceptance by a journal or institution.

## Enquiry steps

1. Share the project purpose, stage, intended audience and institutional context.
2. Explain the question to be solved and what support is already available.
3. Identify ethics, privacy, authorship and approval requirements.
4. Agree a written scope, outputs, timeline, fees and confidentiality terms.
5. Carry out the agreed work and review the outputs with the project owner.
