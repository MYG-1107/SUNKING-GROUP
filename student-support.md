## Support to clarify before enrolment

The support available can differ by programme and provider. Prospective learners should ask for a current support guide and named contact details before starting.

## Common areas of learner support

- **Programme guidance:** understanding entry requirements, learning outcomes and the structure of a course.
- **Orientation:** access to learning systems, study expectations, assessment rules and key dates.
- **Academic guidance:** help finding resources, understanding feedback and planning study.
- **Research guidance:** information about research methods, milestones and the institution's supervision process.
- **Digital access:** instructions for learning platforms and a route for reporting technical problems.
- **Accessibility and adjustments:** how to request reasonable support and who assesses the request.
- **Pastoral or wellbeing support:** available channels and any external or specialist services.
- **Administrative support:** questions about enrolment, records, payments, deferral and completion documents.

## Who is responsible?

If an external university or provider delivers the course, it may be responsible for academic supervision, assessment, student records, formal accommodations and complaints. Ask which support is provided by SUNKING GROUP and which is provided by the institution.

## Get the contact details in writing

Before enrolling, ask for the support email, response hours, escalation route and procedure for urgent issues. Do not assume that a service is available around the clock unless the provider confirms this.
