## Event information

No confirmed public event schedule has been supplied for this page yet. Event details will be published when the organiser, venue or platform, date, speakers and registration arrangements are final.

## What to expect in a complete event notice

A confirmed listing should identify the organiser, event purpose, agenda, speakers and their approved biographies, date and time zone, delivery mode, venue or platform, registration instructions, accessibility arrangements and any fee or cancellation terms.

Where an event includes a certificate, the listing should state which organisation issues it, the participation or assessment requirements and whether it is a certificate of attendance or an assessed qualification.

## Propose a workshop or collaboration

Institutions, professional bodies and companies may contact SUNKING GROUP to discuss a workshop, seminar, conference contribution or learning collaboration. Any use of another institution's name or logo must be authorised and the nature of the relationship explained accurately.

A message expressing interest does not reserve a place. Registration is confirmed only through the process stated in the official event notice.
