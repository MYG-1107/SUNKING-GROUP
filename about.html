About the organisation

Education, people development and consulting

SUNKING is described on its existing website as an international brand working with organisations in the United States, United Kingdom, United Arab Emirates and Europe. The group brings together SUNKING INSTITUTE OF MANAGEMENT (SIM), based in India, and SUNKING CONSULTANTS (SC), operating in the United Arab Emirates.

SUNKING INSTITUTE OF MANAGEMENT focuses on flexible learning, management education and research. Its published approach emphasises experiential learning and programmes intended to help learners strengthen knowledge and skills for the future of business. SUNKING CONSULTANTS lists advisory and training services across business, human resources, management, media and technology education. The existing website identifies SUNKING INSTITUTE OF MANAGEMENT as registered with India’s Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) and MSME, and describes SUNKING CONSULTANTS as registered in the UAE; the current status and exact legal entity details should be checked against official documents before launch.

The group’s published organisational culture highlights respect for learners, employees and clients, work-life balance, gender neutrality and a commitment to continuous development.

Our areas of work

  • Management education, including certificates, diplomas and programmes at postgraduate and doctoral levels, subject to availability.
  • Research-oriented learning and academic development.
  • Leadership and team development training.
  • Business, human resources and management consultancy.
  • Technology education research and development, administrative training and media consultancy.
