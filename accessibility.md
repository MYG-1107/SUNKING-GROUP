## Our approach

The website is designed with responsive layouts, structured headings, keyboard focus indicators, alternative text for images, labelled navigation and a skip-to-content link. These features are intended to improve usability, but they do not establish that every page or interaction is fully accessible.

## Current limitations

No independent accessibility audit or formal WCAG conformance assessment has been completed for this website. Some third-party content, documents, embedded tools or external platforms may have different accessibility characteristics.

## Request information in another format

If a page, document or form is difficult to use, contact the team and identify:

- The page or document involved.
- The device, browser or assistive technology you are using, if relevant.
- The barrier you encountered.
- The format or assistance you would find useful.

Please avoid sending unnecessary medical or sensitive personal information.

## Ongoing improvement

Feedback should be reviewed and used to prioritise improvements. Where an external programme provider controls a learning platform or academic document, its accessibility process may also be relevant. Contact [SUNKING GROUP](/contact/) with questions about a particular service.

This statement should be reviewed as website features and accessibility standards evolve.
