## Why study project management at doctoral level?

Project management is often taught through methods, tools and frameworks, but complex projects also involve people, governance, uncertainty, procurement, technology, organisational politics and strategic alignment. Experienced project leaders may encounter problems that cannot be solved by applying a standard template.

A Doctor of Business Administration in Project Management provides a research route for examining a focused issue in project and organisational practice. SSBM Geneva publishes a **DBA in Project Management** in its online doctoral catalogue and describes it as delivered in collaboration with the International Project Management Association (IPMA). Applicants should verify the current programme page, partnership wording, award details and requirements before applying.

## Possible areas of research

A doctoral project needs a clearly defined question and a method for investigating it. Potential themes might include the relationship between project governance and delivery outcomes, stakeholder engagement in complex transformation, risk communication, the adoption of agile practices in regulated environments, project portfolio prioritisation or the management of benefits after implementation.

These are examples of possible research themes, not a promise that every topic will be accepted. The final topic must fit the programme, supervisor expertise, available evidence and institutional ethics rules.

## From professional experience to a research question

Professionals often begin with a challenge they have seen at work. The next step is to define the issue precisely. Instead of asking "Why do projects fail?", narrow the question to a particular type of project, organisation, process or outcome. Identify what is already known in the research literature and what remains uncertain.

A research proposal should explain the context, problem, question, relevant literature, intended method and expected contribution. The topic must be manageable within the time and resources available. A project that depends on confidential organisational data may require approval from the employer and careful handling of participant information.

## Understand the academic requirements

A DBA is a professional doctorate, not simply an advanced project management certificate. Candidates should expect substantial research reading, critical analysis, method selection, data interpretation and academic writing. Ask how the programme balances coursework with thesis research and what form the final examination takes.

SSBM's doctoral pages describe entry pathways that may involve an MBA or equivalent, or a relevant master's degree with professional experience. The precise rules for the Project Management DBA must be confirmed with the admissions team. Do not assume that holding a project management certification automatically meets doctoral academic requirements.

## Online learning and supervision

The programme is listed in SSBM's online doctoral offering. Confirm the current format and how mentoring, research supervision, seminars and progress reviews are arranged. Ask how supervisors are matched to research topics and how feedback is provided.

A professional doctorate may be completed alongside employment, but candidates should reserve regular research time. Project workloads can fluctuate; build a plan for periods when work deadlines, travel or organisational changes compete with academic tasks.

## Evaluating fees and research costs

Compare tuition with the full cost of completing the doctorate. Depending on the research design, expenses may include travel, interviews, transcription, data access or software. Ask what is covered by tuition and whether an extension, resubmission or additional supervision has a separate cost.

Review the current [SSBM tuition information](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/) and request a formal schedule. Do not base a decision on old discounted prices or third-party listings.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the SSBM Project Management DBA online?

SSBM's catalogue currently lists the programme among online doctoral options. Confirm the latest delivery method and any required synchronous or in-person elements.

### Does the programme have an IPMA connection?

SSBM's published programme information describes the DBA as delivered in collaboration with IPMA. Verify the current scope of the collaboration and any separate membership or certification implications directly with the programme provider and IPMA.

### Can I use my employer's project as a research case?

Possibly, but you need to meet the institution's research ethics, confidentiality and access requirements. Obtain permission before collecting or sharing company data.

### Is the DBA the same as a project management certification?

No. A professional doctorate is an academic qualification with research requirements. A project management certification assesses professional knowledge or practice under its own rules.

## Questions to send to admissions

Ask for the exact award title, academic entry criteria, accepted prior qualifications, research proposal requirements, supervision process, methodology modules, expected completion milestones and total fee. If you have a draft topic, provide a concise description and ask whether it appears to fit the programme's research scope.
