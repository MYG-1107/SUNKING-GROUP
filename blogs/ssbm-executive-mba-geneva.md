## What makes an Executive MBA different?

An Executive MBA is generally designed for professionals who already have substantial work responsibilities and want to deepen their management and leadership capability. The learning often connects business frameworks with the decisions participants face in their organisations. Applicants should still check the programme's specific entry requirements: "Executive" in a title does not automatically mean that every applicant must hold the same number of years of experience.

SSBM Geneva publishes an **Executive MBA (Geneva)** programme. The official page describes a campus-based format and learning themes that include innovation, disruption, leadership and executive management. The programme information has also described core learning across global management, strategic leadership, project management, finance, human resources, marketing, data analytics, digital transformation, operations and accounting. Use the live [Executive MBA page](https://www.ssbm.ch/geneva-executive-mba/) to confirm the latest structure, schedule, intake and conditions.

## Why experienced managers consider executive study

A manager's responsibilities can expand faster than their formal business education. Someone who began in engineering, technology, healthcare, finance or another specialist field may later take responsibility for budgets, hiring, strategy and organisational change. Executive education can provide a way to connect those responsibilities with management theory and structured reflection.

A useful Executive MBA should help learners analyse decisions, not simply collect terminology. For example, a digital transformation initiative involves technology selection, investment, process redesign, employee adoption, risk management and governance. A leadership challenge may involve incentives, communication, organisational culture and performance measurement. A multidisciplinary curriculum can help participants examine these factors together.

## Campus study in Geneva: what to verify

The programme page currently identifies the Executive MBA as a Geneva campus programme. Before making travel or relocation decisions, confirm the teaching calendar, exact campus location, attendance expectations, weekend or weekday schedule, examination arrangements and any required online components.

If you live outside Switzerland, ask how often you must be physically present and whether the programme creates any visa or travel-planning implications. Do not assume that an academic programme automatically provides permission to reside or work in Switzerland. Immigration matters must be checked with the relevant Swiss authorities and should not be inferred from an admissions offer.

## Curriculum: connect subjects to your role

When reviewing the curriculum, consider four groups of capability:

1. **Strategic direction:** global management, strategic leadership and innovation help frame long-term choices and organisational positioning.
2. **Financial and operational control:** finance, accounting, operations and project management help translate plans into budgets and execution.
3. **People and customers:** human resources, sales and marketing connect strategy with employee capability and customer value.
4. **Technology and evidence:** data analytics and digital transformation help managers evaluate technology decisions and use information more responsibly.

The exact module list can change. Ask for the current programme specification and confirm which topics are compulsory, elective or subject to availability.

## Entry requirements and applicant preparation

SSBM Geneva's admissions overview describes undergraduate study as a common requirement for MBA programmes and lists academic transcripts, a CV, identity documentation, a photograph and English-language evidence where required. Some executive routes may require additional postgraduate study or professional experience. Review the current conditions for the Geneva Executive MBA rather than relying only on the general admissions page.

Prepare a clear professional CV that explains your responsibilities, team or project scope, industry experience and academic history. Where you have managed budgets, implemented change or led cross-functional work, describe the context and your contribution accurately. Do not exaggerate job titles or outcomes.

## How to evaluate the investment

Compare the total tuition, application fee, instalment options, travel, accommodation, study materials, time away from work and the opportunity cost of attendance. Ask what student support, academic advising, career services and networking opportunities are formally included. Request all financial terms in writing.

The school publishes current fee information at its [tuition-fees page](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/). Because amounts and payment terms may change, use the official current schedule and obtain a written fee statement for your intended intake.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the SSBM Executive MBA based in Geneva?

SSBM's published programme page identifies it as a Geneva campus programme. Check the current schedule and attendance requirements directly with the school.

### Is an Executive MBA only for CEOs?

No. Executive MBA applicants may include managers, entrepreneurs, functional leaders and experienced professionals who want broader strategic capability. The formal admission criteria still apply.

### Can I take the Executive MBA fully online?

The published Geneva Executive MBA is listed as campus-based. Ask SSBM about the current delivery arrangements and compare it with the school's separate online MBA options.

### Does an Executive MBA guarantee promotion?

No. A qualification can support learning, but career outcomes depend on experience, performance, industry conditions and employer decisions.
