## What does "Flex Bachelor" mean?

A flexible bachelor's programme may combine structured campus learning with online study. The purpose is to provide some in-person experience while allowing learners to complete part of their work remotely. The exact balance, timetable and attendance rules must be confirmed for the specific course.

SSBM Geneva publishes a **Flex Bachelor in Business Administration (Swiss)**. Its public programme information describes a blended structure with a proportion of on-campus learning and a larger online component. The official page should be checked for the current format, location, course sequence, credits and admission conditions. Start with SSBM's [Geneva Bachelor programmes](https://www.ssbm.ch/geneva-bachelor-programs/) and [programme finder](https://www.ssbm.ch/course-search/).

## Why blended study may appeal to students

Some learners value the structure and social experience of campus education but also need flexibility to complete learning away from the classroom. A blended format can be useful when students have family responsibilities, live outside the campus city or prefer a mix of direct interaction and independent study.

However, blended learning is not automatically easier. The online portion requires self-management, reliable technology and the ability to work independently. Campus elements require planning around travel, attendance and fixed sessions. Students should understand the actual timetable before deciding.

## How to assess the learning design

Ask how the online and in-person elements connect. Are campus sessions used for workshops, group projects, presentations or specific modules? Are online sessions live, recorded or a combination? How do students communicate with faculty? What digital platform is used, and how are assignments submitted?

The quality of a blended experience depends on how well the different modes are integrated. It is not enough to know that a programme contains both online and campus components. Understand the learning activities and the expectations for each one.

## A realistic weekly plan

Before applying, map a typical week against your existing responsibilities. Include live sessions, independent reading, assignments, group work, travel time and revision. If the provider offers a sample academic calendar, use it to estimate the workload. If the exact timetable is not yet published, ask when it will be available.

Check whether required campus sessions are concentrated into blocks or spread throughout the term. This affects the practical cost of travel and accommodation. If you live in another country, confirm whether any attendance element has immigration implications and consult official government guidance before making plans.

## Admissions and student preparation

SSBM Geneva describes bachelor-level entry requirements through its admissions information. These may involve an accepted upper-secondary qualification or equivalent, with additional evidence depending on the applicant's education history. International applicants should ask how their qualifications are evaluated and whether documents require translation.

Prepare certificates, transcripts, proof of identity and any language evidence requested. Ask about deadlines, assessment procedures, tuition, payment schedules and refund conditions. If the provider uses a central application system, follow the official process rather than sending personal records through unofficial channels.

## Compare a Flex Bachelor with a fully campus-based route

A fully campus-based course may provide more regular face-to-face contact, while a blended course may reduce the frequency of campus attendance. The right choice depends on learning preferences, travel constraints, timetable, personal responsibilities and the way the programme is actually delivered.

Do not choose solely on the proportion of online learning. Compare academic support, access to faculty, assessment requirements, group interaction, campus resources and the total cost of participation. Ask whether the degree title and academic requirements differ from the related campus programme.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the SSBM Flex Bachelor fully online?

No. SSBM's published description identifies a blended format with an on-campus component. Confirm the current ratio and attendance requirements with the school.

### Can I live far away from Geneva?

That depends on the required campus schedule and any applicable travel or immigration requirements. Ask SSBM for the exact attendance calendar before deciding.

### Is blended learning easier than campus study?

Not necessarily. It changes where and how learning takes place, but students still have to meet the academic requirements and manage their time.

### Where can I confirm current details?

Use the [official Geneva Bachelor programme page](https://www.ssbm.ch/geneva-bachelor-programs/) and ask the SSBM admissions team about the specific intake.
