## What is an Executive DBA?

An Executive Doctor of Business Administration is a professional doctorate aimed at experienced managers and leaders who want to deepen their understanding of complex organisational issues. It combines advanced business learning with research. The exact balance of coursework and thesis work depends on the institution and programme.

SSBM Geneva publishes an **Executive DBA** and describes themes that include strategic leadership, innovation, transformation and executive decision-making. The official page also outlines advanced coursework and applied research components. Review the current [SSBM Executive DBA page](https://www.ssbm.ch/executive-dba/) before relying on any summary of the curriculum or duration.

## Why a senior leader might consider doctoral study

Experienced professionals often face challenges that cannot be resolved through a checklist or a single management model. Examples include managing organisational transformation, developing resilient business models, responding to technological disruption, building ethical governance systems and evaluating complex investments.

A DBA provides a structured environment to investigate one of these challenges. The research should draw on academic literature and suitable evidence, not simply describe personal experience. The candidate must be able to explain the method used, the limitations of the findings and the contribution to professional practice.

## The Executive DBA themes

SSBM's published description refers to strategic leadership, innovation and transformation, and executive decision-making. Its programme information also identifies contemporary themes such as AI, data disruption, automation and ethical challenges. Check the current course list to understand how these themes are taught and assessed.

When comparing modules, look for a connection between leadership theory and practical decisions. Ask whether the programme addresses organisational culture, strategy execution, governance, data-informed management and change. Confirm the scope of the research component and whether the final output is a dissertation or another approved form of doctoral work.

## Does the route fit your career stage?

An Executive DBA may be worth considering if you have substantial professional experience and a clear question that you want to investigate. It may be less suitable if you are mainly seeking a short executive course, a general management qualification or a quick career credential.

Doctoral study requires persistence. You must read critically, write academically, work with evidence and respond to feedback. It is important to identify a research topic that is manageable alongside your professional responsibilities.

## Admission: understand the academic route

SSBM Geneva's general doctoral admissions information describes possible entry routes involving an MBA or equivalent, or a master's qualification combined with relevant professional experience. The precise Executive DBA requirements should be confirmed on the official course page. If your prior degree is outside management, ask how the institution evaluates its relevance and the experience it expects.

Prepare full transcripts, certificates, CV, identity documentation and any other records requested. A strong professional profile does not remove the need to meet formal academic criteria.

## Online delivery and time management

The Executive DBA is listed by SSBM as an online programme. Confirm how live sessions, self-paced materials, assignments and mentoring work. Ask how often you need to communicate with the mentor and what happens if a work commitment disrupts your study plan.

A realistic schedule should reserve weekly research time. Even when coursework is flexible, the thesis requires a continuous effort. Candidates should consider whether they can protect time for reading, writing, data collection and analysis over the full period of study.

## Financial due diligence

Review tuition, payment arrangements, any additional academic charges and the conditions for withdrawal or extension. Ask what is included in the fee and whether resubmission or additional supervision creates further costs. Use the current [SSBM fee information](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/) and obtain a written statement for the programme.

## Questions senior applicants should ask

- What exact qualification and award title will be granted?
- What academic and professional background is required?
- How does the curriculum differ from the Global DBA?
- What research methods are covered?
- How is doctoral supervision arranged?
- What is the expected workload alongside employment?
- What evidence is required for the final thesis or assessment?
- What support is available if research access becomes difficult?

## Frequently asked questions

### Is an Executive DBA only for executives with a C-suite title?

Not necessarily. The formal requirement is determined by the programme. Applicants should ask how SSBM defines relevant professional experience.

### Is the SSBM Executive DBA online?

The official page currently lists an online format. Confirm the current teaching and supervision arrangements.

### Is an Executive DBA the same as an Executive MBA?

No. An Executive MBA is a management master's qualification; an Executive DBA is a professional doctorate with advanced research requirements.

### Will the DBA guarantee consulting or academic work?

No. A doctorate may develop research and professional capabilities, but employment outcomes are not guaranteed.
