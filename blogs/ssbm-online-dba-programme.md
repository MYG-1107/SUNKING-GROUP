## What is an online DBA?

An online Doctor of Business Administration is a professional doctoral route delivered through digital learning systems. It can allow experienced professionals to continue working while undertaking doctoral-level coursework and research. Online delivery does not reduce the academic responsibility: candidates must still meet research standards, produce original work and complete the institution's assessments.

SSBM Geneva publishes several online DBA routes, including a DBA dissertation track and a Global DBA. The official programme pages explain the curriculum and admission criteria for each. Start with the [Online DBA programme information](https://www.ssbm.ch/online-dba-program/) and the current [SSBM programme finder](https://www.ssbm.ch/course-search/).

## What does DBA research involve?

A DBA normally begins with identifying a researchable problem in business or professional practice. The candidate then develops a research question, examines existing academic work, chooses methods, gathers or analyses evidence and explains the findings. The final project or dissertation must be coherent, ethical and supported by appropriate evidence.

A workplace issue can be a useful starting point, but it is not automatically a doctoral research question. "Improve company performance" is too broad. A more focused question might investigate how a defined leadership practice influences the adoption of a process change in a particular type of organisation. The final scope depends on the literature, research access and the institution's academic guidance.

## Online study: flexibility and discipline

Online delivery may help professionals avoid relocation, but doctoral work requires regular protected time. Reading research papers, designing methods, collecting data and writing chapters cannot be completed only in occasional spare moments. Candidates should plan a weekly schedule and consider how travel, family responsibilities and work deadlines may affect progress.

Ask how the online programme combines self-paced materials and live sessions. Confirm how often students meet mentors, how feedback is provided, whether there are research workshops and how progress is monitored. The quality and availability of supervision are important considerations for a research degree.

## Compare the DBA routes

SSBM's current catalogue includes different doctoral pathways. A dissertation track, Global DBA, Executive DBA and specialist DBA can have different course structures, research expectations, eligibility requirements and fees. Do not choose only by the word "online" or by the programme title.

Read the full programme page and ask the admissions team to explain the difference between the routes. Confirm whether the programme requires a dissertation, thesis or applied research project, how the supervisor is assigned and what constitutes successful completion.

## Admission requirements and academic background

SSBM Geneva's general admissions information describes doctoral entry routes based on an MBA or equivalent qualification, or a relevant master's degree combined with professional experience. It also lists supporting documents such as academic transcripts, a CV, identity document and photograph. Requirements can differ by route and must be confirmed with the institution.

If your master's degree is outside management, ask how the school evaluates its relevance and what evidence of professional experience is required. If you hold a professional doctorate or overseas qualification, provide the exact title and awarding institution. Do not assume that the programme will treat every degree as equivalent.

## Prepare for the research journey

Before applying, consider the topic you may want to investigate. Identify the practical problem, the people or organisations affected and the type of evidence that might be available. Think about whether you can access records, interview participants or collect data ethically. Your employer may need to approve access to internal information.

A doctoral proposal is not simply a business plan. It should explain the issue, why it matters, what is already known, the research question, a possible methodology and how the work may contribute to knowledge or practice. Ask the institution what proposal format it expects at admission.

## Financial and completion planning

Review tuition, currency, instalments, additional academic fees and refund rules. Also consider research-related costs such as travel, data collection, transcription or specialist software if relevant. Ask whether the published fee covers supervision, learning resources and assessment.

Use the provider's current [tuition-fee information](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/) and request written confirmation. Programme duration is not the same as a guaranteed completion date; research progress depends on the approved plan, candidate workload, feedback and institutional rules.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I complete an SSBM DBA while working?

The online format may make it possible to combine study and employment, but the workload still needs careful planning. Confirm the expected commitment and live-session requirements.

### Do I need an MBA to apply?

SSBM's doctoral admissions information describes an MBA or equivalent as one common route, alongside certain master's-plus-experience pathways. The admissions team must confirm your specific eligibility.

### Is the DBA a research degree?

A DBA is a professional doctorate with significant research expectations. Check the exact dissertation or thesis requirements for the selected programme.

### Who awards the qualification?

Check the exact award title and awarding institution in the programme regulations and formal offer.
