## Step 1: Choose the exact programme

Start by identifying the qualification and programme you want to study. SSBM Geneva's catalogue includes bachelor's, master's, MBA, DBA/doctorate, Doctor of Education and postdoctoral options. Delivery formats can include online, campus, live virtual or blended study, depending on the course.

Use the [official SSBM programme finder](https://www.ssbm.ch/course-search/) to search by programme level, subject and format. Open the individual course page and note the exact title, awarding institution, location, delivery mode and current curriculum. A broad search result is only a starting point.

## Step 2: Read the programme-specific entry requirements

Do not rely on one general admissions summary for every qualification. A bachelor's programme, an MBA, a DBA and an Ed.D. can have different entry criteria. Read the relevant course page and compare it with the [SSBM admissions overview](https://www.ssbm.ch/admissions-overview/).

If you have an international qualification, a degree in a different subject or an unusual academic route, ask the admissions team how it will be evaluated. Provide the exact qualification title and the name of the awarding institution. Avoid statements such as "equivalent to a master's" unless the institution has confirmed that assessment.

## Step 3: Prepare your documents

The documents requested depend on the programme. SSBM's published admissions information commonly refers to academic certificates and transcripts, a CV or résumé, an identity document and a photograph. English-language evidence may be requested from some applicants. Doctoral routes may ask for additional professional or research information.

Create a checklist based on the current programme instructions. Ensure scans are clear and complete. Keep names and dates consistent across documents. If a certificate is not in English, ask whether a certified translation is required. Do not edit academic documents or alter the information on them.

## Step 4: Prepare your CV and application information

Your CV should provide a clear record of education, employment and relevant experience. Explain responsibilities and achievements accurately. For postgraduate and doctoral programmes, make the relationship between your experience and the course clear.

For a doctoral application, you may also need to describe a potential research interest. Define the issue you want to investigate, why it matters and what evidence might be available. A detailed research proposal may be required, but the format should follow the institution's instructions.

## Step 5: Submit through the official Student Application System

SSBM Geneva directs applications through its central Student Application System. Use the official [application portal](https://apply.ssbm.ch/) and follow the current [application instructions](https://www.ssbm.ch/apply-to-ssbm/).

Complete all required fields, upload the requested documents and review the information before submitting. Save a copy of the confirmation and any application reference. Do not assume that an initial contact form or conversation with a partner is equivalent to a formal application.

## Step 6: Respond to follow-up requests

The admissions team may ask for clarification, further documents or an interview. Monitor the email account associated with your application and respond within the stated timeframe. If you are uncertain about a request, contact the institution through the official channel.

Keep copies of the documents you submit and correspondence you receive. If there is a change to your contact details, notify the admissions team so that you do not miss important communication.

## Step 7: Review the formal offer carefully

If you receive an offer, check the programme title, academic conditions, delivery format, intake, payment deadlines and any outstanding requirements. An offer may be conditional on additional documents or evidence. Make sure you understand what must be completed before enrolment.

Do not pay based only on an informal message or an old brochure. Use the institution's official payment instructions and confirm any unusual request directly with SSBM through a verified contact channel.

## Step 8: Check tuition and cancellation terms

Review the current [SSBM tuition-fee schedule](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/) and ask for a written breakdown of the full amount payable. Check the currency, instalments, additional charges, scholarship conditions, refund rules, withdrawal process and deferral options.

For campus study, budget separately for travel, accommodation and living costs. If you are an international applicant, check visa and residence requirements with the relevant authorities. University admission does not itself give you immigration permission.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Choosing a programme based only on its title or headline advertisement.
- Using outdated tuition information.
- Assuming an application has been accepted when only a preliminary enquiry was made.
- Uploading incomplete or unclear academic records.
- Assuming a partner can guarantee admission or a scholarship.
- Paying before reading the formal offer and refund rules.
- Treating admission as a guarantee of a visa, job or residence permission.
