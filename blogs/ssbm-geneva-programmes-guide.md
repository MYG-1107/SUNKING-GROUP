## Start with the qualification you need

People searching for **SSBM Geneva programmes** may be at very different points in their education. A school-leaver may be comparing undergraduate business degrees, a graduate may be considering a specialist master's qualification, and an experienced manager may be exploring an MBA or professional doctorate. The right starting point is not the programme name that sounds most impressive. It is the level of qualification that matches your prior education, experience and intended learning outcome.

SSBM Geneva's public programme finder groups its catalogue into Bachelor, Master, MBA, DBA/Doctorate, Doctor of Education and Postdoc categories. It also allows visitors to filter options by campus, online delivery, blended study and subject area. That makes the official [SSBM programme finder](https://www.ssbm.ch/course-search/) the best place to verify the current title and availability of a course before you make a decision.

## Understand the main programme levels

### Bachelor's programmes

A bachelor's degree is an undergraduate route for learners who have completed an accepted secondary-school qualification or equivalent. Business administration provides a broad introduction to areas such as management, finance, marketing, people, operations and organisational decision-making. SSBM also publishes a Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence, which has a distinct technology focus. Applicants should compare the exact award title, curriculum, teaching location, entry criteria and language of instruction.

### Master's programmes

A master's programme is a postgraduate route that normally follows an undergraduate degree. SSBM lists management-focused master's study, including a Master of Science in Management. The curriculum and format depend on the specific programme page. Before applying, check the academic level, credits, assessment methods, dissertation or research requirements, and whether the programme is delivered online or in Geneva.

### MBA programmes

The Master of Business Administration is intended for learners who want to develop broader or more advanced management capability. SSBM's catalogue includes general and specialist MBA pathways, including the Online Global MBA, an Executive MBA in Geneva and an Online MBA in Strategic Intelligence. Programme themes may include strategy, leadership, innovation, finance, data analytics, digital transformation and executive decision-making. An MBA is not automatically the right next step for every graduate; applicants should review the admission criteria and the professional purpose of each route.

### DBA and other doctoral programmes

The Doctor of Business Administration is a professional doctorate that centres on advanced business inquiry and applied research. The exact balance of coursework, research modules, mentoring and dissertation work differs across DBA pathways. SSBM also publishes a Doctor of Education (Ed.D.) for education and learning professionals. These awards have different purposes and should not be treated as interchangeable.

### Postdoctoral and further-learning options

A postdoctoral research fellowship is intended for people who already hold a doctoral qualification and want to extend their research activity. It is not a substitute for a first doctorate. SSBM also lists certificates, diplomas and executive education, which may suit specific professional-development goals without being equivalent to a full degree.

## Online, campus and blended study

The word "online" should not be treated as a complete description of the learning experience. Check whether recorded lessons, live sessions, group projects, campus attendance, examinations or in-person activities are required. A campus programme in Geneva may have a defined timetable and intake calendar. An online programme may provide more flexibility, but learners still need to meet assignment deadlines, participate in required activities and complete formal assessments. A blended programme combines modes and may require travel.

Use the individual programme page to confirm the format currently offered. Do not assume that every subject is available in every format, or that a programme's delivery arrangements remain unchanged from one intake to another.

## A practical comparison framework

Before shortlisting programmes, make a simple comparison table with these fields:

| What to compare | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Awarding institution and exact award title | Confirms which institution is responsible for the qualification |
| Entry requirements | Helps you check whether your previous education and experience fit |
| Curriculum and learning outcomes | Shows whether the subjects match your objective |
| Study format and location | Helps you plan work, family commitments and travel |
| Duration and study workload | Helps you assess whether the programme is realistic |
| Fees and additional charges | Gives you a complete financial picture |
| Assessment and research requirements | Explains how you must demonstrate learning |
| Deferral, cancellation and refund rules | Helps you understand the terms before paying |

Do not rely on a short advertisement or search result to settle these questions. Ask for the current programme specification and written fee information.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does SSBM Geneva offer online programmes?

The official catalogue lists online options across several programme categories. Availability is programme-specific, so confirm the exact format on the selected course page.

### Can I apply directly through SUNKING GROUP?

SUNKING GROUP can provide initial programme guidance. SSBM Geneva controls its admissions process and directs applicants to its official Student Application System.

### Are programme fees the same for every course?

No. Fees vary by programme and delivery format. Review the latest [official tuition-fee information](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/) and request a written breakdown before making a payment.

### Where can I compare current programmes?

Use the [SSBM Geneva programme finder](https://www.ssbm.ch/course-search/) and the school's [admissions overview](https://www.ssbm.ch/admissions-overview/).
