## Start with the requirements for the exact MBA

Before preparing an application, identify the specific SSBM Geneva MBA you want to study. A general online MBA, a specialist MBA and a Geneva campus Executive MBA may not share identical entry rules or application timelines. A programme title or broad university admissions page should not be treated as a personal assessment.

SSBM Geneva directs applicants to its central Student Application System and publishes a general [admissions overview](https://www.ssbm.ch/admissions-overview/). The page describes a recognised undergraduate degree or equivalent as a common entry route for MBA programmes. It also refers to academic transcripts, a CV or résumé, an identity document, a photograph and English-language evidence where required. Executive routes may have additional postgraduate study or professional-experience criteria.

## A document checklist

### Academic certificates and transcripts

Prepare certificates and official academic transcripts for previous qualifications. The admissions team may need to understand the institution, qualification title, grades, study dates and the level of the award. If your documents use a grading system that is unfamiliar internationally, keep a clear explanation or transcript supplement available if requested.

Do not change or reinterpret grades yourself. Submit accurate copies and follow the provider's instructions for certified translations or attestations. If a document is missing, ask the admissions team what alternative evidence it will accept.

### CV or résumé

Your CV should show education, employment history, responsibilities and relevant professional development. For an executive programme, explain the nature of your leadership or management responsibilities accurately. Include measurable achievements only where you can substantiate them.

A clear CV helps the admissions team understand your background. It should not be written as an advertisement or include inflated job titles.

### Identity document and photograph

SSBM's published general information refers to an identity document and a photograph. Check which identity documents and image formats are accepted for your application. Upload sensitive documents only through the official application channel or another method specified by the institution.

### English-language evidence

English requirements can depend on the applicant's educational background and programme. The official admissions page references English proficiency for some applicants. Check the accepted tests, scores, exemptions and document validity period for the exact MBA. Do not assume that a score accepted by another university will be accepted by SSBM.

## How the admissions assessment works

Admission is more than a document upload. The institution reviews the applicant's background against the selected programme's entry rules. It may ask for clarification, additional records or an interview. The final decision belongs to SSBM Geneva's admissions process.

Applicants with a non-standard route should be especially careful. A professional qualification, incomplete degree, unrelated undergraduate subject or extensive work experience may be relevant, but none automatically guarantees entry. Ask whether recognition of prior learning or another assessment route is available and request the policy in writing.

## Common application mistakes to avoid

- Applying to a programme before confirming its exact entry criteria.
- Uploading incomplete transcripts or certificates.
- Using inconsistent names or dates across documents.
- Sending unreadable scans or photographs of documents.
- Assuming prior work experience automatically replaces an academic requirement.
- Relying on unofficial advice about English-language exemptions.
- Paying a deposit before reviewing the formal offer and financial terms.
- Confusing a general enquiry with a completed application.

A short pre-submission review can prevent avoidable delays.

## Apply through the official system

SSBM Geneva provides an official [Student Application System](https://apply.ssbm.ch/) and an explanation of its [application process](https://www.ssbm.ch/apply-to-ssbm/). Read the instructions for the selected course, complete the application accurately and upload the requested records. Use the institution's admissions contact if you need clarification about missing documents, eligibility or the next step.

After submitting, monitor the email account used in the application and respond to requests within the stated timeframe. Keep a copy of your submission and any correspondence. Do not interpret a form confirmation as an offer of admission; wait for formal communication from the school.

## Fees and offer review

Before accepting an offer, check the complete tuition amount, currency, payment dates, instalment terms, additional fees and refund conditions. Compare the written fee schedule with the amount shown in any payment instructions. If something is unclear, ask the institution to explain it before you transfer money.

Use the official [SSBM tuition-fee page](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/) for the current published schedule. The amount may differ by programme and format, and financial terms can change.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is a bachelor's degree required for an SSBM MBA?

SSBM's general admissions overview describes an undergraduate degree from a recognised university or equivalent as a common MBA entry route. The school must confirm how your specific qualification is assessed.

### Do I need work experience?

Requirements vary by programme. Some executive routes may require additional postgraduate education and professional experience. Check the exact programme page.
