## Why professionals consider an online MBA

An online MBA can appeal to professionals who want structured management education without relocating or leaving employment. The format may help learners organise study around work and personal commitments, but flexibility should not be confused with a light workload. Students still need to read, attend required sessions, complete assignments, collaborate where required and prepare for assessments.

SSBM Geneva publishes an online programme catalogue that includes MBA options. The current programme finder shows both general and specialist subjects and allows visitors to filter by online format. Start with the [official SSBM programme finder](https://www.ssbm.ch/course-search/) and confirm the exact course page before deciding.

## What might an online MBA cover?

An MBA is a broad management qualification. Depending on the specific programme, learning may address strategy, leadership, finance, marketing, people management, project management, operations, business analytics and digital transformation. Specialist routes can place greater emphasis on an area such as strategic intelligence.

A broad curriculum is useful because managers rarely make decisions in just one business function. A pricing decision, for example, may affect revenue, customer positioning, operations, staffing and cash flow. Management education can help learners examine those connections rather than treating each department in isolation.

The exact curriculum matters more than a list of fashionable subject names. Ask whether modules are compulsory or elective, how the learning is assessed, whether there is a capstone or final project, and how current examples are used in teaching.

## Is online MBA study suitable for you?

Online study may suit you if you can plan independent work, use digital learning systems confidently and protect regular study time. It can be challenging if your work schedule changes every week, you need continuous face-to-face prompting or you have limited access to a reliable internet connection.

Before enrolling, create a realistic weekly calendar. Include reading, recorded learning, live classes if applicable, assignment preparation and revision. Consider work travel, family responsibilities and peak periods in your job. If a provider describes a programme as flexible, ask what flexibility actually means: self-paced access, fixed live sessions, multiple assessment windows or a particular study sequence.

## Admission requirements and documents

SSBM Geneva publishes requirements by programme. Its admissions overview describes an undergraduate degree or equivalent as a common entry route for MBA programmes, alongside academic transcripts, a CV or résumé, identity documentation, a photograph and English-language evidence where required. Certain executive routes may specify further education and professional experience.

These are programme-level guidelines, not a personal eligibility decision. The institution decides how it assesses international qualifications, subject backgrounds, work experience and English proficiency. Review the current [SSBM admissions overview](https://www.ssbm.ch/admissions-overview/) and confirm the exact criteria for the MBA you are considering.

Prepare clear digital copies of your documents. Keep the names and dates consistent across the CV, application and certificates. If your name has changed or your transcripts use a different format, ask the admissions team how it wants the discrepancy documented.

## Compare the programme before comparing the price

A low headline fee does not necessarily represent the full cost of study. Compare the total tuition, currency, payment schedule, additional charges, optional activities, resit rules and refund conditions. Also check the programme's expected duration and the academic support included.

Use SSBM Geneva's live [tuition-fee page](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/) for current published information. Ask for written confirmation of the total amount payable for your chosen programme and intake. Do not rely on an old brochure or a search-result snippet to make a financial commitment.

## Questions worth asking

- Which exact MBA award will I receive, and which institution awards it?
- Is the programme fully online, or are live or in-person sessions required?
- What are the core modules and available electives?
- What is the expected weekly study commitment?
- How are assignments, examinations and projects assessed?
- What academic support and student services are included?
- What is the complete fee, including any additional charges?
- What happens if I need to defer, withdraw or repeat an assessment?

Writing these questions down makes conversations with providers more productive.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I study an MBA while working full-time?

Many online MBA formats are designed with working professionals in mind, but you must check the actual workload, deadlines and live-session expectations of the selected course.

### Does an online MBA mean there are no deadlines?

Not necessarily. Online programmes can include fixed assignment dates, live sessions, examinations and research milestones. Confirm the academic calendar.
