## What is a Global DBA?

A Global Doctor of Business Administration is a professional doctorate that addresses business and management challenges in an international context. It can be relevant to experienced professionals who work across markets, lead diverse teams or investigate strategic questions that affect organisations operating in different regions.

SSBM Geneva publishes an **Online Global DBA**. Its official programme description highlights innovation and disruption, leadership and executive management. It also describes advanced coursework and applied research components. Review the current [SSBM Global DBA page](https://www.ssbm.ch/online-global-dba/) for the programme structure, admission criteria, research milestones and fee information.

## Global business challenges and doctoral research

Global organisations operate in complex environments. A decision about supply chains may involve several countries, different regulations and currency exposure. A digital transformation programme may affect employees, customers and partners across multiple cultures. Leadership decisions may need to account for varied communication styles and local business conditions.

Doctoral research can help a professional investigate one of these issues in a systematic way. The candidate defines a focused problem, reviews relevant theory, chooses a suitable method and analyses evidence. The result should be more than a description of what happened in one company. It should offer a defensible insight that relates to existing knowledge and professional practice.

## Themes described by SSBM

The published Global DBA information refers to strategic leadership, organisational transformation, data disruption, emerging technologies and ethical challenges. It also lists research methodology, proposal development and a thesis as part of the doctoral journey. Course availability and the final sequence should be confirmed through the current programme specification.

Think about how these themes relate to your experience. A business leader might investigate the adoption of AI in a regulated service, the resilience of a cross-border supply chain or the implementation of sustainable business practices. A consultant might explore how organisations evaluate transformation programmes or measure the effect of leadership interventions.

## What makes a research project manageable?

A strong DBA topic is specific enough to investigate and relevant enough to matter. Avoid starting with a broad theme such as "globalisation" or "leadership". Identify the particular decision, organisational setting or group that you want to understand. Then ask whether data can be accessed ethically and whether the project can be completed within the programme's time and resource limits.

Consider whether the research requires confidential company information, interviews with employees or access to clients. Obtain permissions before collecting data. If the organisation cannot approve access, the topic may need to be redesigned.

## Who should investigate this route?

A Global DBA may be relevant to senior managers, entrepreneurs, consultants and experienced professionals who want a research-based route for examining complex business questions. Candidates should be ready for academic reading, research design, critical analysis and sustained writing.

SSBM's admissions information describes doctoral routes that commonly require an MBA or equivalent, or a relevant master's degree with professional experience. The exact entry criteria for the Global DBA must be checked directly. Experience is not a substitute for meeting formal academic requirements.

## Online study and supervision

Online doctoral study requires a reliable plan for communication and feedback. Ask how often you will meet your mentor or supervisor, what feedback arrangements apply, whether research workshops are available and how progress is reviewed. Confirm whether any live sessions or in-person components are required.

The programme's flexible format should not be interpreted as an absence of deadlines. Research milestones and academic regulations still apply. Clarify the expected schedule for the proposal, methodology, data collection, progress reviews and final thesis.

## Fees and doctoral commitment

A professional doctorate is a significant academic and financial commitment. Review the total fee, currency, instalment terms, what supervision includes and whether any additional charges apply. Ask about deferral, extension, withdrawal and resubmission conditions. The current [SSBM tuition-fee page](https://www.ssbm.ch/tuition-fees/) should be used together with written confirmation from the school.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is the SSBM Global DBA online?

The official Global DBA page currently lists an online format. Verify any live-session requirements and the current programme calendar.

### Is it different from an Executive DBA?

The names suggest different emphases, but applicants should compare the actual curriculum, research structure, target audience and assessment rules on each official page.

### Do I need an MBA?

SSBM's doctoral admissions information describes an MBA or equivalent as one typical route, with other pathways potentially available based on master's qualifications and experience. Confirm your individual eligibility.

### Can a Global DBA guarantee an international leadership position?

No programme guarantees a role or promotion. The qualification may support learning, but career outcomes depend on experience, performance and market conditions.
